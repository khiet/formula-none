Star, item slot icon

Three icons for the star

The star's effect on the kart stays as it is. Only the slot's character changes. I recommend the unicorn. Each icon is drawn the way item-sprites.ts draws, shining through the same 2 Hz loop, large, in the slot held and firing, at 64 px, and beside the other items.

Today's icon, for comparison

The safety car in star-and-air-horn.html. Its shine is confined to the bodywork, and at 64 px it is mostly a small car, which is a poor signal in a racing game full of cars.

A, recommended, your idea

Unicorn

The drawing
A chibi unicorn standing side on, in a chequered saddle cloth, with a rainbow mane, tail and horn that flow through the loop.
Why it works
The rainbow belongs to it, so the icon and the kart's shine say the same thing. It has a face, like the cone, and nothing else on the grid looks like it. It's the funniest thing to see land in the slot.
What it costs
It is the one item that isn't from the paddock. Keep all four hooves down and leave out any shield, so it can't be mistaken for Ferrari's prancing horse.
B, the shiniest

Podium mirror ball

The drawing
A faceted mirror ball on a short chain, each tile a step along the rainbow, with white glints. No face: the tiles carry it, as the cork carries the bottle.
Why it works
Polygons suit it better than any other object here, and the whole ball shines. It reads clearly at 64 px and belongs at the podium party with the champagne.
What it costs
It says party, not power. Nothing in it tells a new player they've become fast and untouchable.
C, the best fit with the paddock

Holographic helmet

The drawing
A race helmet side on with a shining shell and an iridescent visor strip, and determined eyes behind the visor.
Why it works
A helmet means protection, so it comes closest to saying invincible. It is a real paddock object, like the bottle, cone and drone, and drivers do wear one-off special helmets.
What it costs
It's the safest and least memorable of the three. The game's drivers are bare-headed, so nothing on the road echoes it.
