Why it is subtle

    Chase view

    A rough chase camera 2.2 m up on the approach to Brands Patch's hairpin two: 12 m of asphalt, 6 m of run-off each side, the post 3 m short of the crossing on the right. Passes alternate sides, 24 m wall to wall in 1.6 s, as the sim rolls them.

    Wheel look

    Each drawn at your picked size beside a kart at the same scale, at a quarter of its real spin so the face reads. All four are mesh and View changes only.

    Wheel size

    The size is the sim's reach, WHEEL_RADIUS_M: a kart spins out when its box comes within it of the wheel's centre, and the View draws the wheel that size so the wheel you see is the wheel that hits. Growing it is a rules change: the tuning constant, the generated Tuning.ts, the replay fixtures if one meets a pass, and the bots' margin.

    Marshal post

    Each runs on the cycle in the bar above: up from a second before the pass to its end, down while quiet. All four are scenery and View changes; none touches the sim.

    Telegraph

    Danger also comes from seeing it coming. Any mix of these goes with any look; they show in the chase view.
