Homing drone

Three ways to turn the homing projectile from a gull into a drone. Each is built from Three primitives in the palette, the way homingGullMesh is, nose toward +z and hovering at the projectile's 0.6 m. Drag any model to turn it. The slot icon beside each is the mesh drawn flat, as the item slot draws the gull today.

The designs

Today: gull

homingGullMesh
drag to turn

White body, grey wings up, orange beak, red eyes locked on. Static: nothing on it moves.

A. Camera quad

recommended
drag to turn

A trackside broadcast FPV drone gone rogue: carbon X frame, red canopy, four orange motors under spinning rotor discs, and a camera pod whose red lens is the locked-on eye.

  • Reads as "drone" from any angle; the four rotors are the signifier, and they show best from the chase camera, which sees it from behind.
  • Belongs at a race: real F1 broadcasts fly FPV camera drones.
  • Widest of the three (1.0 m rotor to rotor); flat, so it is thin side on.

B. Seeker dart

fixed wing
drag to turn

A small strike drone: white fuselage, swept grey wings, orange twin fins, a pusher prop at the tail and a red nose whose seeker light sweeps side to side across a dark visor.

  • Says "this is coming for you" loudest; the shape points at its target.
  • Narrowest; slips cleanly between karts.
  • Closest to a missile, so less playful than the cone; from behind it is mostly a prop disc.

C. Grumpy eye

character
drag to turn

A faceted white ball with one big red eye under the grumpy cone's brows, a ducted orange fan on top and a flame-yellow thruster underneath; it bobs as it hovers.

  • Matches the item set's character: the cone has a face, and so does this.
  • The slot icon is the strongest of the three: one eye, face on.
  • Reads as "robot" before "drone"; the face hides from the chase camera behind it.

On the road

All four at the projectile's height over asphalt, from roughly where the chase camera sits. Flip them to see one coming at you.

What changes in the code

Recommendation: A. The rotors carry the word "drone" without help, and the chase camera mostly sees the projectile from behind, where A still reads and C's face does not.

  • item-meshes.ts: homingGullMesh becomes homingDroneMesh; the file header's "the cone and the gull" follows.
  • race-view.ts: the gulls pool becomes drones, and spins its rotors each frame. That is new: the gull had no animation, so the pool needs a per-frame hook.
  • item-sprites.ts: the homing drawing is redrawn as the slot icon here.
  • palette.ts: GULL_EYE renamed for the drone.
  • docs/style-guide.html: the gull card, the slot row and the palette roles, in the same PR.
  • The sim, the protocol and the glossary are untouched: it stays the homing projectile.
