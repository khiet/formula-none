Runaway wheel danger spec

The runaway wheel and its marshal post read as scenery: a grey wheel on grey asphalt (1.2:1) and a yellow flag over a yellow run-off (1.1:1). This spec records the redesign chosen to make them read as danger: a burning wheel almost three times the size, Oyaji from Purrjump as the marshal, and four tells that show the pass coming. The cycle (7 s period, 1 s warning, 1.6 s pass) and the crossing are unchanged.

Behaviour

  • Rolls across from wall to wall as today, turning by the ground it covers and bouncing 0.5 m three times a pass. The bounce is drawn only; the sim rolls it on the ground.
  • The flames always point away from the way it rolls, so they flip with each pass's direction.
  • A kart whose box comes within 1.2 m of its centre spins out, by no one, as today. The bot's avoidance box (bot.rs, reach plus half the kart's length and width) grows with it.

Flags

  • Two, one in each hand, each 0.9 x 0.62 m on a 1 m pole #cfd2d6, rippling as they fly outward.
  • Yellow SIGNAL_YELLOW.mid with black #1f1f22 diagonals: stripes 0.09 m wide every 0.2 m, the same as the lane painted across the road, so flag and road say the same thing.
  • Not a racing flag, so it can't be read as one: the yellow with red stripes that means a slippery surface is avoided on purpose.

The post

  • Scenery in the glTF, where it stands today: 3 m short of the crossing, 3 m into the outside run-off.
  • A white platform 1.4 m wide and 1 m tall (MARSHAL_POST_WIDTH_M 1.0 → 1.4, MARSHAL_POST_HEIGHT_M 2.0 → 1.0) with an orange band 0.12 m deep below its top and the orange lamp on its face to the track.
  • An amber beacon on the track-side corner, built in the View: dark #7a5a14 while quiet; while the flag is up, lit #ffb020 with its glow and a beam that sweeps side to side at 11 rad/s.
