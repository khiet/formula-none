One car, every livery

The race render is Blender's workbench view of the exported model with the slot's paint and driver parts applied, the same camera for all eight. The generated poster is every face of the glb that shows from the kart's right, pitched down four degrees so each top face is a lit strip and the far wheels a sliver of tyre, shaded by its normal against one light in three tones, in painter order; the same 250 units a metre, 520 x 380 frame and ground line as the tracing. Where the two differ, the glb is right: the eyes are the build's boxes on the head's forward flat, the hair its shells and balls, the halo its hoop and legs.

What changed

  • Source: one car, drawn once in build.py. The poster follows the glb; a change there cannot leave it behind, and CI fails on a stale copy.
  • Shading: three tones from each face's normal against one light, so the lit strips along the tops and the facets on the head come from the geometry rather than being drawn in.
  • Colours: the tracing's rim and tyre greys had drifted from the build; the generated kart takes every fixed colour from the glb's materials, copied into palette.ts and held to the glb by a test.
  • Head: the eyes show as the build's boxes on the forward flat, a sliver of white and iris, not the round eye the tracing drew; the light stubble, one forward-facing face on the chin, shows nothing from the side. Both follow the build until the build changes.
  • Wheels: four groups, not two, each about its own axle; the far pair peeks over the near as a sliver of tyre.
  • Frame: unchanged, so the Room Scene's rows, the podium widths and the runners' CSS stay as they are.
