Oyaji the marshal

The marshal on the post is Oyaji from Purrjump, rebuilt in Formula None's flat, faceted style. Pick one of three marshals and one of three flags; any marshal holds any flag. The rest is already settled: the hot wheel at 1.2 m radius, the painted lane that flashes red, the wheel waiting on the wall, and dust at each bounce.

Purrjump's scripts/cats.mjs draws Oyaji as a 16 x 16 pixel sprite. What carries over:

  • Bald head with a shine, grey hair only at the sides, heavy brows, a wide grey mustache.
  • A round belly, a white running vest, blue shorts and sandals.
  • His two faces: calm (idle) and wide-eyed with brows up (fall). The calm face shows while the wheel is quiet; the alarmed one shows from the flag going up.

In the race he is built in code from flat-shaded primitives, like the flag and the item characters, because he moves. The post box stays in the track's glTF. His colours are Purrjump's, apart from the hi-vis orange, which is the post's existing lamp orange #ff8800.
