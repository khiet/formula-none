Foundations

One language, the car and its driver: chibi low-poly for the world, the posters and the Instrument panel (ADR 0034), with a livery as a palette (ADR 0035). Everything below is built from these rules, colours and one face.

World and posters / ADR 0034, 0035, 0036

Chibi low-poly

  • Short wheelbase, oversized wheels, rears bigger than fronts, all inside the sim's 1.2 x 1.8 m box.
  • Flat-shaded facets: every panel in a lit and a shaded tone of one colour. Flat materials, no textures.
  • Wheels and heads are polygons: octagon tyres with three spokes, a faceted head about 0.6 m across.
  • Drivers race bare-headed: big eyes, short jaw, skin and hair swapped on one shared head.
  • A livery is primary, secondary and accent, plus a key colour for every single-colour use.
  • No team name, crest, sponsor or race number on a car.
Instrument panel / ADR 0037

The car's facets, on the View

  • Readouts sit on faceted plates: chamfered corners, a lit, a mid and a shaded band of one colour, and the posters' hard ink drop. No outline.
  • White values, grey labels, a 0.1 em ink drop under each, in the pixel face.
  • Your readouts wear your livery: the position plate in primary, flags and hubs in accent. Other karts show in their key colour.
  • Polygons, not pixels: shift lights are hexagons, map markers octagons, the tyre gauge is your car from above with your driver's hair.
  • The item characters are the View's meshes drawn flat, so the slot and the road show one cone, one gull, one snail.
  • Sized in HUD units, max(0.55rem, 1.5vmin), so the panel scales with the window and still reads in a small one. Blinks hold steady under reduced motion.

Palette

Colours by role. The Instrument panel's and the items' colours live in apps/web/src/palette.ts, the liveries in apps/web/src/livery.ts; repo-checks fails when this page disagrees with either. A new colour joins a role here before it ships.

Runaway wheel

apps/web/src/item-meshes.ts runawayWheelMesh, runawayWheelFlames; wheel-tells.ts
Size
1.2 m radius (WHEEL_RADIUS_M in tuning.rs), 2.4 m across and 0.96 m wide
Decided
ADR 0041, ADR 0042, ADR 0044, the danger spec

The hot wheel, grown to the sim's reach: an eight-facet tyre in WHEEL_TYRE #26262a, dark so it reads on the grey asphalt, with a red-hot rim on each face, ROCKET_FLAME #ffd23f at the hub to ROCKET.mid #d00a12 at its edge and unlit so it glows, three spokes in ROCKET_STRAP #3a3d42 and a flame-yellow hub. The View rolls it where the sim has it on the approach to Brands Patch's hairpin two: in from one wall, twice off the walls and out through the far one, facing the way it rolls. It turns by the ground it covers and bounces 0.5 m twice each leg, starting over at each wall; a kart it reaches spins out.

Three flame tongues burn off its back, WHEEL_FLAME #ff8800 and see-through round a flame-yellow core, unlit and flickering; they point away from the way it rolls, so they swing round at each wall hit. It leaves grey WHEEL_SMOKE #8a8d94 puffs behind it that rise and fade. Where it lands from each bounce and at each wall hit it kicks up five puffs of the run-off's sand dust #d8c08a that spread and fade over 0.45 s, and at each wall hit eight flame-yellow sparks fly 1.5 m back over the track off the wall where the tyre strikes, fading over 0.3 s. The wall hits and the landings are read from the path the sim reports: a leg starting over, and half way through one.

Through the second the flag is up before a pass it waits, burning, on top of the wall it will roll in from, on the crossing's line, rocking 0.15 rad either way about its axle, and drops to the road as the pass starts. The sim reports the foot of that wall; the View finds the wall's top in the track's glTF. Up there no kart reaches it, and it hits nothing.

It is built in code like the items, not in the track's glTF, because it moves. The bounce is drawn only: the sim rolls it along the ground, so the reach it tests is the one the player sees where the wheel meets the road.

Hazard zone

assets/tracks/trackgen/layout.py hazard_zones, assets/tracks/build.py; apps/web/src/hazard-zone.ts
Size
Wall to wall, 18.4 m deep: 9.2 m either side of the crossing (WHEEL_LANDING_SPREAD_M plus WHEEL_RADIUS_M, the sidecar's hazard_half_depth_m)
Decided
the danger spec

Paint over the whole area the runaway wheel can reach: a 1 m band of chevrons across each end, 1 m stripes of gold #ffd23f and black #111111 leaning 0.6 m across the band, and a light yellow tint between, the gold at 14% over the asphalt (#807a70) and the sand (#ebc86b). Scenery in the track's glTF, a hair over the ground like the kerbs; no surface tag, so it drives as what it lies on.

From the flag going up to the end of the pass the View washes it red, SIGNAL_RED.mid #e8483a, pulsing between 20% and 60% at 14 rad/s; quiet, the wash is gone. The next landing point is not shown, on purpose.

Always there, so a racer learns the spot by the second lap; the flash says when.

Marshal post

assets/tracks/trackgen/layout.py marshal_posts; apps/web/src/item-meshes.ts marshalMesh, hazardFlag, marshalBeacon
Size
1.4 x 1.4 x 1 m, 3 m short of the wheel's crossing on the outside run-off; each flag 0.9 x 0.62 m on a 1 m pole
Decided
ADR 0041, ADR 0045, the danger spec

A low white platform, banded orange #ff8800 0.12 m deep under its top, with an orange lamp on its face to the track: scenery in the track's glTF. The View stands Oyaji, the marshal, on its top facing the track, and an amber beacon on its track-side corner.

His two hazard flags are SIGNAL_YELLOW.mid #f5c542 with HAZARD_FLAG_STRIPE #1f1f22 diagonals 0.09 m wide every 0.2 m, leaning down away from the pole, on a pole in WHITE.shaded #cfd2d6. Flying, the cloth ripples out from the pole. Quiet, they lean furled against his stool.

The beacon is BEACON_OFF #7a5a14 while the wheel is quiet. From the flag going up to the end of the pass it is lit BEACON_LIT #ffb020, glowing with a halo, and two BEACON_BEAM #ffcf4a beams sweep round at 11 rad/s.

Don't give the flags red stripes: yellow and red is the racing flag for a slippery surface, and a hazard flag must not read as a racing flag.

Oyaji

apps/web/src/item-meshes.ts marshalMesh
Size
About 1.75 m standing; head 0.68 m across
Decided
ADR 0045, the danger spec

The marshal, adapted from the author's own Purrjump character and drawn as Purrjump draws him: bald with a MARSHAL_SKIN.lit shine, grey MARSHAL_HAIR side hair, heavy MARSHAL_BROWS brows and a wide MARSHAL_MUSTACHE mustache on a faceted MARSHAL_SKIN head, a round belly in a MARSHAL_JACKET hi-vis jacket with a MARSHAL_REFLECTIVE band over a MARSHAL_VEST vest, a white towel round his neck, MARSHAL_SHORTS shorts and MARSHAL_SANDALS sandals. Chibi like the drivers; built in code from flat-shaded primitives, because he moves, and the sun shades him.

His pose follows the wheel's kind. Quiet: seated on a green MARSHAL_STOOL folding stool, knees apart, sipping from a white cup as steam rises; calm, flat brows and dot eyes. Warning: on his feet, arms up, a flag in each hand waved out of phase at 14 rad/s through 34° either side of 18° out; panic, brows high, eyes wide, mouth open and MARSHAL_SWEAT sweat flying off. Rolling: as the warning, hopping up to 0.18 m.

The code calls him marshal; "Oyaji" is his name here and in assets/LICENSES.md only. He stands only on the marshal post.
