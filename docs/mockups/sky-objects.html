Sky Objects

The flying prop for each course that has none yet, chosen from six candidates (the earlier version of this page, in git history). Bell Deer keeps its airship. Each prop follows the airship's rules from ADR 0050 and scenery-motion.ts: it is scenery on the View's own clock, the sim never sees it, it has no shadow and no sound, its lowest point stays 30 m over every road, and it wears only sponsors already in sponsors.py.

Monarch Helicopter

A white helicopter on a slow orbit between the casino and the harbour mouth, its rotors always turning. It is red and white, the colours of the real principality, and those reds are already the course's own: the kerb-red of its kerbs on the belly stripe and the fin. The sponsor is BOX BOX BOX LOGISTICS because its ink is red; a sponsor's ink comes with its name, so a red helicopter cannot wear LATE BRAKING BANK.

Size
12.8 m long, 11 m rotor
Flies
Once round every 2 min at 35 m, nose dipped 5°
Moves
Main and tail rotors turn, the same kind of motion as the Circuit Wheel's rim
Wears
BOX BOX BOX LOGISTICS on the cabin, ink-red
Build
The cabin, boom and fin in props.py. Two rotor nodes for scenery-motion.ts to turn, found by name like the wheel's rim.
wall body beam-shade underside kerb-red stripe, fin carbon glass, skids, rotors ink-red lettering

Goldstone Display jets

Three red display jets in an arrowhead, each trailing a smoke ribbon, over the old airfield's hangars. It makes a fast flypast every lap or so, not a slow drift.

Size
9 m jets, 50 m smoke ribbons
Flies
Once round a wide circle every 45 s at 60 m
Moves
The whole formation; the smoke is fixed ribbons that ride with it, not particles
Wears
No lettering (it is too small at 60 m)
Build
Three small hulls plus ribbons under one node, flown on the airship's circle.
kerb-red jets wall flash, lead smoke carbon canopy ink-blue upper smoke kerb-red lower smoke
