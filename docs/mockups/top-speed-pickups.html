Top speed pickups

Five things to lie on the straights in place of the tyre. The rule from ADR 0055 stays as it is in every one: collect up to ten, each raises top speed 1%, a spin-out costs three, the pickup is gone for 3 s. Only the object, its mesh, its gauge and its word in the glossary change. Each card shows the pickup, a line of them on a straight from above, and the Instrument panel readout at seven of ten.

Coin

Mario Kart's answer

On the straight

Instrument panel

x07 / 10

Fit

  • Every player already knows what a coin does: pick it up, go faster, lose some when hit.
  • A spin-out can spill three coins as a spray the View draws, the clearest loss cue of the five.
  • Cheap mesh: an octagonal prism, spun on its vertical axis.
  • The most borrowed of the five; the game reads less like its own thing.
  • Nothing about it says Formula. The embossed F does the work.
2

ERS cell

Recommended

On the straight

Instrument panel

ERS

Fit

  • Real F1 language: harvested energy makes the car faster. Fits the poster-F1 theme.
  • The gauge is a ten-segment battery, so "how full is my set" reads at a glance without a number.
  • Go-green on asphalt is the highest-contrast pickup of the five.
  • Players may expect to spend the charge on a button, as in real ERS. It is passive here.
  • Green collides with the start lights' go colour; the bolt keeps it distinct.
3

Fuel can

On the straight

Instrument panel

FUEL

Fit

  • A needle from E to F is the most familiar gauge there is.
  • Poster red and gold, already the palette's loudest pair.
  • Backwards to motorsport: in F1 more fuel is more weight, so slower.
  • Empty sounds like the kart should stop, and at zero it drives at full base speed.
  • Red cans read as hazards beside the red-and-white kerbs.
4

Spanner

On the straight

Instrument panel

SETUP 7 / 10

Fit

  • Pit-crew theme: each spanner is one more tweak to the kart's setup.
  • The setup level can show on the kart as an extra part per few spanners (a wing, then a scoop).
  • Thin and grey; the hardest of the five to spot on asphalt at 30 m/s.
  • Losing three to a spin-out reads oddly: the kart unbuilds itself.
5

Sponsor sticker

On the straight

Instrument panel

Fit

  • The count shows on the kart itself: each sticker lands on the bodywork, so rivals can read who carries a full set.
  • A spin-out peels three off, visibly, in the chase camera.
  • Most its own idea; nothing else in the genre does it.
  • Why a sticker makes a kart faster needs a sentence ("sponsors pay for speed").
  • Most View work: ten decal slots per kart on top of the livery.
