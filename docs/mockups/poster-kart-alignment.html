Title Scene runners

Both runners wear their slot's livery (Notte, then Argento, as the Title Scene picks slots 1 and 2), and their wheels turn with the ground they cover: one turn of the rear wheel per 0.41 s for a 10 em kart crossing a 1440 px window in 5 s, the front wheel smaller by the build's 0.9 and that much faster. Forwards is clockwise, the kart facing right. With reduced motion preferred, nothing moves.

What changed

  • Proportions: the wheelbase, the oversized octagon wheels (rear 0.30 m, front 0.27 m), the big head and the halo at chin height are the build's; the old poster kart was a longer, lower car on small wheels.
  • Paint: primary tub, engine cover, shoulders and wing planes; secondary floor, sidepods, airbox and front wing; accent fin, collar, nose tip and endplates; carbon halo and pillar; rim-grey spokes, no accent hub, as the View has none.
  • Title Scene: the runners wear the full livery instead of the key colour alone, so the Title, the Room, the podium and the race show one car.
  • Motion: each wheel is its own group turning about its centre; the runners roll as they cross.
  • Size: the side view is 520 x 380 rather than 520 x 260, so the Room Scene's rows grow to 7 em to hold the taller kart.
