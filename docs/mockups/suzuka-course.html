Brands Patch, Suzuka cut

A first draft of the race track reworked after Suzuka: the figure eight, the esses, Dunlop, the two Degners, the hairpin, 200R, Spoon, the back straight over the bridge, 130R, the chicane and the final curve. Corner angles are measured from the real circuit; straights are cut to kart length and radii opened so no two legs come within 32 m of each other except at the bridge. The map is laid live in this page from the same turtle steps as trackgen/layouts, so editing SPEC or PLACEMENTS in the source redraws it.

Reference: the real circuit

5807 m, same orientation as the draft (north up), from the public bacinger/f1-circuits GeoJSON. The draft keeps every corner's direction and roughly its angle, but at 1306 m it cannot keep the real gaps: 130R and the chicane sit further from Dunlop than they do at Suzuka.

How the draft was made

  1. Measured the heading change of each corner on the real outline (turn 1 and 2 together turn 187 degrees right; Spoon 209 left; the whole lap nets zero, as a figure eight must).
  2. Laid the corners with Turtle.arc at those angles and kart-sized radii, hairpin 17 m, chicane 12 to 13 m, nothing tighter than the hairpins on Brands Patch would allow with the walls 12 m off the centreline.
  3. Annealed radii and straight lengths so the lap closes, the back straight crosses the underpass at close to 90 degrees, and every other pair of legs keeps 32 m between centrelines (8 m of grass between walls).
  4. Rounded to whole metres and degrees, then built the layout through trackgen.layout.build: it closes, 12 item boxes, 2 boost pads, 2 kerbs, 1 crossing, deck at 5.0 m over the underpass at 0.0 m.

Elevation

The bridge is a Hill of 5 m over 110 m centred on the crossing, 14% at its steepest (Brands Patch's hill is 2 m over 50 m, 13%). The underpass stays at ground level. The jump ramp sits on the flat between Spoon's exit and the climb, so a kart lands before the bridge starts to rise.

Layout draft

Drop-in shape for assets/tracks/trackgen/layouts/, in the style of brands_patch.py. It builds and closes as written; the map above draws from the same numbers.

"""Suzuka cut: a figure eight of about 1306 m.

The start/finish straight runs south-east into the first curve and turn 2,
then the esses, Dunlop and the two Degners; the run to the hairpin passes
under the bridge, then the 200R, the double-apex Spoon and the back straight,
which climbs over the bridge before 130R, the chicane and the final curve.
Tyre walls stand inside turn 2, the hairpin and Spoon's second apex, sausage
kerbs inside esses 3 and the chicane, the runaway wheel crosses the run from
the bridge to the hairpin, its marshal post on the outside, and the jump sits
at Spoon's exit. The scenery: grandstands along the outside of the start
straight and the hairpin exit, hoardings round Spoon, 130R and the final
curve, and the gantry over the line.
"""

from ..layout import (
    BoostPad,
    Decor,
    Grandstands,
    Hoardings,
    ItemRow,
    Kerb,
    Layout,
    TyreWall,
    WheelCrossing,
)
from ..path import Hill, Ramp, Turtle

KERB_INSIDE_M = 4.9
TYRE_WALL_INSIDE_M = 4.5

path = (
    Turtle(x=0.0, y=0.0, heading_deg=-50.0)
    .straight("start", 60.0)
    .arc("first-curve", 50.0, -51.0)
    .straight("turn-2-approach", 26.0)
    .arc("turn-2", 21.0, -111.0)
    .straight("esses-run", 27.0)
    .arc("esses-1", 30.0, 57.0)
    .arc("esses-2", 20.0, -71.0)
    .straight("esses-link", 9.0)
    .arc("esses-3", 21.0, 55.0)
    .arc("esses-4", 21.0, -122.0)
    .arc("dunlop", 34.0, 102.0)
    .arc("dunlop-rise", 41.0, 58.0)
    .arc("degner-1", 24.0, -70.0)
    .straight("degner-link", 53.0)
    .arc("degner-2", 18.0, -75.0)
    .straight("underpass", 85.3)
    .arc("hairpin-kink", 43.0, -28.0)
    .straight("hairpin-approach", 12.0)
    .arc("hairpin", 17.0, 180.0)
    .straight("hairpin-exit", 21.0)
    .arc("two-hundred-r", 43.0, -99.0)
    .straight("spoon-entry", 8.0)
    .arc("spoon-1", 17.0, 109.0)
    .straight("spoon-link", 20.0)
    .arc("spoon-2", 29.0, 108.0)
    .straight("back-straight", 190.14)
    .arc("one-thirty-r", 52.0, 40.0)
    .straight("chicane-approach", 13.0)
    .arc("chicane-in", 12.0, -76.0)
    .arc("chicane-out", 13.0, 86.0)
    .arc("final-curve", 40.0, -92.0)
    .close("grid")
)

# Where the back straight passes over the underpass, metres along each leg.
BRIDGE_UNDERPASS_S = 573.8
BRIDGE_BACK_STRAIGHT_S = 1020.7
BRIDGE_DECK_M = 5.0
BRIDGE_HALF_SPAN_M = 55.0
RAMP_HEIGHT_M = 1.5
RAMP_LENGTH_M = 12.0

back = path.segment("back-straight")
hairpin = path.segment("hairpin")
ramp_start = back.s_start + 14.0

LAYOUT = Layout(
    name="suzuka",
    path=path,
    asphalt_half_width=6.0,
    runoff_width=6.0,
    # Fifteen keeps every checkpoint at least 24 m from the bridge on both legs.
    checkpoint_count=15,
    tyre_walls=[
        TyreWall(path.segment("turn-2").s_mid, offset=-TYRE_WALL_INSIDE_M),
        TyreWall(hairpin.s_mid - 8.0, offset=TYRE_WALL_INSIDE_M),
        TyreWall(hairpin.s_mid + 8.0, offset=TYRE_WALL_INSIDE_M),
        TyreWall(path.segment("spoon-2").s_mid, offset=TYRE_WALL_INSIDE_M),
    ],
    item_rows=[
        ItemRow(path.segment("esses-run").s_mid),
        ItemRow(path.segment("degner-link").s_mid),
        ItemRow(back.s_end - 15.0),
    ],
    boost_pads=[
        BoostPad(path.segment("dunlop-rise").s_mid),
        BoostPad(path.segment("hairpin-exit").s_mid, offset=3.0),
    ],
    kerbs=[
        Kerb(path.segment("esses-3").s_mid, offset=KERB_INSIDE_M),
        Kerb(path.segment("chicane-in").s_mid, offset=-KERB_INSIDE_M),
    ],
    # The hairpin turns left, so its outside is the right.
    wheel_crossings=[WheelCrossing(BRIDGE_UNDERPASS_S + 28.0, outside=-1)],
    elevation=[
        Ramp(ramp_start, ramp_start + RAMP_LENGTH_M, RAMP_HEIGHT_M),
        Hill(
            BRIDGE_BACK_STRAIGHT_S - BRIDGE_HALF_SPAN_M,
            BRIDGE_BACK_STRAIGHT_S + BRIDGE_HALF_SPAN_M,
            BRIDGE_DECK_M,
        ),
    ],
    decor=Decor(
        hoardings=[
            Hoardings(("grid", "start"), side=1),
            Hoardings(("spoon-2",), side=-1),
            Hoardings(("one-thirty-r",), side=-1),
            Hoardings(("final-curve",), side=1),
        ],
        grandstands=[
            Grandstands(("grid", "start"), side=1),
            Grandstands(("hairpin-exit",), side=-1),
        ],
        gantry=True,
    ),
)

Engineering notes

  • The crossover breaks spline progress. Track::progress_at takes the nearest centreline point in x and z only, so a kart at the bridge reads either leg: about 574 m or 1021 m. Checkpoints, positions, bots (bot.rs reads progress for its line) and the wrong-way logic all lean on it. Height alone does not settle it either: the deck is 5 m up and the run-off reaches 12 m from the centreline. This needs a sim change and an ADR before the track can ship (question 2).
  • The surface probe is fine under the deck. surface_at casts down from 0.1 m above the kart, so a kart in the underpass sees its own asphalt, not the bridge.
  • Checkpoints avoid the bridge. They are evenly spaced; with 15 the nearest is 24 m from the crossing on either leg, so no checkpoint's wall-to-wall span sits under or over the other road. Changing the lap length means rechecking this (the stats line above does).
  • Walls on the deck. The export stands walls on the run-off edge following the centreline's elevation, so the deck gets parapets for free; nothing yet draws the bridge's piers or the deck's underside. Those are Scenery, so they leave the sidecar alone.
  • The wheel's hazard zone. The crossing is 28 m past the bridge and 18 m before the hairpin kink. If the zone reaches back under the deck it will paint there, which may read oddly from the chase camera.
  • Blast radius of reworking in place. About 20 test files and the e2e autopilot name brands-patch, and two replay fixtures (brands-patch-chicane, brands-patch-first-corners) record its geometry. The scripted followers in the sim tests (hairpin lines, Brands straight timings) will need new targets.
  • Lap length. 1306 m against Brands Patch's 797 m, so a lap takes about 1.6 times as long at the same pace.

Decided

  • Rework in place. The brands-patch layout is replaced under the same id; every Brands test and both replays move in the same change.
  • Keep the full course, race two laps. The layout stays as close to the real circuit as kart scale allows; LAPS_PER_RACE goes from 3 to 2, so a race is about 2610 m against today's 2390 m.
  • Spline progress searches near the last reading. Each kart's progress is found within a window of centreline around its previous progress, with a whole-lap search on a respawn. Snapshots already carry spline_progress, so prediction has the same anchor as the server. Needs an ADR.
