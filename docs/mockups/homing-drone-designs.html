Homing drone

The homing projectile is drawn as a camera quad: a trackside broadcast FPV drone gone rogue. It replaces the gull on the road and in the item slot. While it chases its target it tilts into each turn: left side down to go left, right side down to go right. Nothing in the sim or the protocol changes; it stays the homing projectile.

What moves

  • Rotors spin, diagonal pairs in opposite directions.
  • The tally light blinks at 1.5 Hz.
  • The body bobs 2 cm as it hovers.
  • The whole drone tilts into each turn, below.

Tilt

The rule

  1. Into the turn. Turning left, the drone rolls about its own nose so its left side drops; turning right, its right side drops. Level when it flies straight.
  2. Turn means facing. The turn is how fast the drone's facing yaws, in radians per second, measured frame to frame. The View already turns it to face its target every frame, so that facing is the one the tilt follows. The difference between frames is wrapped into (-pi, pi], so crossing the yaw seam never reads as a full spin.
  3. In proportion, then capped. The tilt grows with the turn rate and reaches its cap of 30 degrees at 2 rad/s. A kart at top speed steers at 1.2 rad/s, so a drone following one through an ordinary corner tilts about 18 degrees. Only a hairpin or the snap onto its target at lock range hits the cap.
  4. Eased, never snapped. The tilt eases toward that value with a time constant of 0.12 s, so the jitter in interpolated snapshots never shows as a wobble, and the drone rolls back to level over about a third of a second once the turn ends.
  5. The whole drone tilts. Rotors, canopy, arms and camera pod roll together. Roll comes after facing: face the target, then roll about the nose.
  6. A new drone starts level. One that has just been fired has no previous facing, so it shows no tilt until its second frame.

Tilt against turn rate

Dashed: the steer rate of a kart at top speed. Flat beyond 2 rad/s: the cap.

Where it lands in the code

  • item-meshes.ts: homingGullMesh becomes homingDroneMesh, returning the drone and the rotors to spin; the file header's "the cone and the gull" follows.
  • race-view.ts: the gulls pool becomes drones. It is the first pool that keeps state from frame to frame: last facing and current tilt for each drone, plus the rotor, tally and bob animation. meshPool stays as it is for the cones.
  • The tilt as a pure function, roughly tilt(previousYaw, yaw, previousTilt, seconds), so the rule above can be pinned by unit tests without drawing anything.
  • The cap, the full-tilt rate and the ease are View constants beside the drone, not in tuning.rs: they change nothing the sim decides.
  • item-sprites.ts: the homing drawing redrawn as the slot icon above. palette.ts: GULL_EYE renamed DRONE_EYE.
  • docs/style-guide.html: the gull card becomes the drone card, and the slot row and the palette roles follow, in the same PR.

One thing to settle in the build

The snapshot lists projectiles in the order they were fired, with no id. If an earlier one hits, every later one moves up a place, and a tilt kept by index would pass to a different drone. Options: match drones frame to frame by nearest position, or reset to level whenever the count drops. Nearest position is my pick: it keeps the tilt with the drone that earned it. Resetting makes the remaining drones snap level for a moment.
