Item spec, full design

The star and the Air Horn

The two items to add to the boost, the hazard and the homing projectile. The rules below are written against the sim and the audio as they stand. The star's icon was chosen in star-icon-alternatives.html and the Arcade sound set in star-and-horn-sounds.html. The numbers are still starting values for a bot sweep and a feel check, and no game code has changed yet.

Item one

The star

For 6 s the kart is faster, takes no harm from items or the runaway wheel, ignores the off-track cap, and spins out every kart it touches. Kart and driver shine through the rainbow the whole time, as they do under Mario Kart's star.

  • Same on every screen: the colour is worked out from star_s in the snapshot, so every client shows a kart in the same phase and the sim stays free of presentation.
  • Flashing limit: a 2 Hz colour sweep stays under the limit of three flashes a second. Check it on the real View, with a star kart filling the screen, before shipping.
  • Materials: dressKart copies only the painted materials, and carbon, tyre and rim are shared between karts. The shine needs a kart's own copy of every material, or one star would light up the whole grid.
  • ADR 0024: it says the characters have no idle animation. The shining mirror ball is an exception, so the star's ADR needs to supersede that line for this item.
  • No mirror ball on the road: the kart shines and stays a kart. As with the boost's bottle, the ball appears only in the slot, so it needs no mesh.

Where the existing rules would get it wrong

It cannot be a long boost

Handling::boost replaces the boost under way, so the first mini-turbo or pad would end a run. The star needs its own star_left_s timer.

It has to raise the cap

A boost forces speed every tick, whatever the brake says. Six seconds of that would make a hairpin undriveable. The star raises top_speed, so braking still works, and a one-off 0.5 s kick gives the jump you feel on firing.

It has to skip the bump

Under ADR 0021 the kart that drives in loses speed, and a spun-out kart stands locked. For the run, the star kart's collider ignores other karts, and the sim tests hits with reaches against the star kart's centre. No contact pair means no bump for prediction and server to disagree over.

Bots and network

  • Bots: a bot fires a star as it fires a boost, on the first straight. During the run it stops waiting for the wheel and stops steering around karts (kart_in_the_way), or it would never hit anyone.
  • Prediction: the owner's client predicts the speed and the pass-through from the fire. Only the server spins a kart out, and clients learn of it from the snapshot, as they do with a drone.
  • Known rough edge: other karts are drawn 100 ms in the past, so a hit on a slow kart can land about 3 to 4 m before the star visibly arrives. A spark burst should cover it, and the smoke test should confirm that.
Item two

The Air Horn

One blast, on the press, 7 m around the kart. Karts in reach spin out, and hazards and homing projectiles in reach are used up. It is the only item you save and time, and its best moment is destroying a drone just before it hits you.

Where the existing rules would get it wrong

Distance alone reaches too far

Bell Deer's crossover puts one road 5 m over the other, so a 7 m sphere blasts through the bridge. Goldstone's and Monarch's closest sections are 17.8 m apart centre to centre, so a kart on the inside edge can reach across the barrier. Requiring the target to be within 15 m of spline progress fixes both, with the lap's wrap accounted for.

Nothing warns of a drone

Neither the Instrument panel nor the audio cues mark an incoming drone, and a 0.39 s window can't be met blind. The horn needs a warning: a rising beep and a slot-side marker once a drone chasing your kart is within 40 m, about 2.2 s out. Snapshots already name each drone's target, so this is client code only, and it helps every kart.

Bots can't see drones

bot::drive reads karts, not projectiles. To start with, a bot fires when a kart is in reach, or after 10 s. It doesn't defend. Adding that means passing the projectiles in, which can wait for feel testing.

What players see and hear

  • On the road: a flat orange shockwave ring grows to 7 m in about 0.25 s, on every screen, so the reach can be learned by eye.
  • On the Instrument panel: the sprite stays still when held, as ADR 0024 asks, and the slot flashes on firing like any other item.
  • Sound: the 0.55 s Air Horn blast, heard by distance from the firing kart, and the drone warning's beep. Both are specified under Sound.
  • Network: no new snapshot field. The ring runs off the existing ItemFired, and server-race.ts must start acting on it for other karts as well as the local one.
Sound

The Arcade set

The star's music tells the driver the run is still going, and its last second tells them it is ending. It is original, in the race music's key and tempo (D minor, 160 bpm), where 4 bars take exactly 6 s. None of it borrows from Nintendo's star theme.

0 sStar run, last second shaded6 s

The script at the bottom of this page is the synthesis to port. The engine isn't included, so the game will sound busier than this.

The five files

FileLengthPlays whenWhat it is
star-music6 sFrom the fire until star_s runs outFast pulse-wave arpeggios that climb an octave halfway through, a square-wave lead, a triangle bass, a tick and a snare. In the last second everything drops out but 22 notes tumbling downward.
star-fire0.4 sThe own kart fires a star, in place of item-fireA two-octave rising run of square-wave notes.
star-end0.24 sThe own kart's run ends, unless it ended at the finishThree falling square-wave blips.
air-horn0.55 sAny kart fires the horn, in place of item-fire for the own kartTwo square waves a fifth apart, sagging in pitch, then bit-crushed.
drone-beep0.07 sRepeats while a drone chasing the own kart is within 40 mOne square-wave beep. It repeats every 0.35 s at 40 m, closing to 0.1 s at the kart, and its pitch rises by half.

All five are 16-bit mono PCM at 22050 Hz, written by assets/audio/build.py with pnpm export:audio. The drone beep wasn't one of the three sets. It is written in the same style because the drone warning needs a sound. The levels are tight at the fire: star-fire and star-music peak at 0.96 together at the SFX level, and in the worst case could reach 1.17 with the race music before it fades, plus the engine. Above 1.0 they clip, so check this in the game and lower star-fire if it does. The blast is safe at 0.69 over the race music.

Toggles and other karts

  • SFX toggle: all five files sit under it, including the star music, because that music tells you something about the race. A player who mutes the race music still hears a run lasting.
  • SFX off: no star music plays, so the race music must not go silent. A run never leaves a player with no music at all.
  • Other karts' stars: only the nearest star kart's music plays, joined at its point in the run, and none plays during your own run. Up to seven at once would be noise. Other karts' star-fire and star-end don't play, and the race music doesn't change.
  • Other karts' horns: every blast plays, whoever fired it, since each is short.
  • By distance: full level within 10 m, falling in a straight line to silent at 60 m, through a 1200 Hz lowpass so it sits behind the own kart's sounds. No stereo panning. These are starting values. The "from another kart" buttons above preview 45 m, which is 30% level.

Where the existing audio would get it wrong

Every item plays one fire sound

soundCues pushes itemFire whenever the slot empties. It has to pick the cue from before.item, or a star and a horn would each sound twice.

The race music has no level of its own

The music source connects straight to the toggle's gain, and settings() overwrites that gain's value. Silencing the race music needs a second gain between them, or flipping a toggle during a run would bring it back.

Reconciliation can raise star_s

A correction can push the predicted timer up a little, and a restart on any rise would stutter the music. Only a rise of more than 0.5 s counts as a second star. Small changes leave the playing source alone.

The audio hears only the own kart

ADR 0025 has it read the own kart's readings, so SoundReading and soundCues can't see another kart's star or horn. Those cues need a new input from the race readout, and the star's ADR has to extend that rule. Engines stay the own kart's alone.

The star at a full purse is 1.375 times tuned top speed, which is under HOMING_SPEED_FACTOR (1.6) and the interpolation's teleport limit, so those bounds need no change. The new constants get const asserts in the same block as the others. How the costs work out: a spin-out's 68 m is 1 s stopped (30 m) plus the 2.5 s ramp back to top speed (37.5 m), and the drone's 0.39 s is 7 m at the 18 m/s it closes on a kart at 30 m/s.

Build

One issue and one PR each, star first

Star

  1. A new ADR: extends ADR 0021's contact rule and ADR 0025's own-kart audio rule, supersedes ADR 0024's no-animation line for this item, and cites star-icon-alternatives.html for the icon and star-and-horn-sounds.html for the sound. A glossary entry for the star.
  2. Sim: Item, Item::code and Item::rolled in lib.rs; star_left_s in the readout and state hash; the cap and off-track change in handling.rs; the immunity checks at the hazard, wheel and projectile hits; constants and weights in tuning.rs; the bot rules in bot.rs.
  3. Protocol: the Item enum, the conversions and star_s on the kart snapshot, then pnpm generate:protocol and pnpm build:bridge.
  4. Audio files: port star-music, star-fire and star-end to assets/audio/build.py, run pnpm export:audio, and commit the output. Rebuild the music if STAR_S changes.
  5. Web: the sprite in item-sprites.ts, item-reel.ts, the ring in instrument-panel.ts, per-kart materials in kart-model.ts, the shine and sparkles, the plate and course map, and the rules under Sound in audio-cues.ts and audio.ts.
  6. Close out: re-record the replay fixtures, run a 200-seed bot sweep for DNFs and stuck karts, update docs/style-guide.html, smoke test a star run, and listen to one over the engine for clipping and fatigue.
