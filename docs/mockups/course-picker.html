A draft of the Title Scene with the three courses on it: Bell Deer (after Suzuka), Monarch (after Monaco) and Goldstone (after Silverstone). The way in stays as it is on the left, with the audio toggles under it; the course sits on the right as three flags, and the one picked shows its map, its name and its numbers below them. The pick goes with Create room and Solo race; a player joining by code races the room's course. The maps are laid live from the same turtle steps as the course drafts (Bell Deer, Monarch, Goldstone). Behaviour

  • Who picks. The player on the Title Scene, for the room they create or the solo race they start; that player is the room's creator. Joining by code ignores the pick.
  • What it sends. Create room carries the course to the server, which keeps it on the room and puts it in Countdown{track}, which already names the track. Solo race loads the picked course locally.
  • Default. Bell Deer on a first visit; afterwards the last course picked, saved beside the audio toggles and the nickname.
  • Preview. The map is the course's centreline from its sidecar, drawn the way the course map draws it (asphalt over a white wall line) with the start line and a gold dot lapping it. Under it: lap length, laps, the tightest corner and the longest straight.
  • Addressable. The flags are radios named course with the values bell-deer, monarch and goldstone, so the Playwright lobby flow can pick one by name as it does the audio toggles.

Open questions

  1. Where the creator picks the course.
    1. (Recommended) On the Title Scene, as drawn: one pick covers Create room and Solo race, and it sits beside the audio toggles you already set there.
    2. In the Room Scene, before Start: the creator can change it while friends join, but Solo race needs a picker of its own.
  2. Whether the creator can change the course once the room is open.
    1. (Recommended) No: the Room Scene shows it read-only. Changing it means a new room.
    2. Yes: the course badge in the Room Scene is a button for the creator, and the server tells everyone.
  3. Whether the Title Scene remembers the last course.
    1. (Recommended) Yes, saved in the browser like the audio toggles.
    2. No, Bell Deer every time.
  4. What the preview shows.
    1. (Recommended) The flat map with the lap dot and four numbers, as drawn: cheap, and it reads at a glance.
    2. A slow flyover of the course rendered in Three.js: richer, but it loads the track before anyone races it.
