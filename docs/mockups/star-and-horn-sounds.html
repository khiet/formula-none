Sound, the star and the Air Horn

Three sound sets

Each set has 6 s of music for the star, so you can hear the run lasting, plus a sound when it fires, a warning in the last second, a sound when it ends, and an Air Horn. I recommend the disco set. It suits the mirror ball, and the record slowing down is a clear warning that the run is ending. I built these from their waveforms and can't hear them, so the choice needs your ear.

The shaded end of each waveform is the last second of the run. The race music plays at its game level here, but the engine is not included, so the game will sound busier than this page.

The sets can be mixed, for example disco music with the stadium horn. All the music is original and synthesised here, in the race music's key and tempo (D minor, 160 bpm), where 4 bars take exactly 6 s. None of it borrows from Nintendo's star theme.

Once you've picked

  1. Add the chosen synthesis to assets/audio/build.py and run pnpm export:audio for four new files: star music, star fire, star end and Air Horn.
  2. Play the star music from star_s in the snapshot, so a player reconnecting mid-run joins it at the right point. A second star restarts it from the top, and the finish fades it out.
  3. Put the star music under the SFX toggle, with the other loops. It tells you something about the race, so it should still play for a player who has muted the music. The race music's level changes only when the music toggle is on.
  4. Other karts' stars and blasts play by distance, as the "From another kart" buttons preview. This is new for these cues, since the audio engine plays only the own kart's sounds today.
  5. If STAR_S changes, rebuild the music to match its new length.
