Drift spark release

Sparks are style 6, Grinder, split by tier. Orange is the quiet one: thin streaks at 60/s, a 6-spark pop, no glow, no bounce. Red is the full Grinder: 90/s at 7.5 m/s, glow, bounce, a 20-spark pop. Colours are option B's, cooling over each spark's life.

Each loop drifts, charges orange at 0.3 s and red at 0.6 s, releases at 1.4 s into the red tier's 1.0 s mini-turbo, then coasts. The three views differ only in what the release adds.
