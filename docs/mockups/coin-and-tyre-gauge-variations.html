Coins

1. Ten pips the purse as a shape

The take: the cap is the row's length, the count is how much of it is gold, and there are no digits at all. A spin-out knocks three pips out where you can see it, and a full purse is a full row. Trades: it sits right above the twelve shift lights, two rows of pips; the coins are gold and face on, the lights carbon and hexagonal, which may or may not be enough.

2. One stamped coin a count, not a fraction

The take: one coin with the count stamped on its face in ink, the way the gear sits in its plate. The cap is not shown: at ten a FULL tag appears beside it. The smallest readout of the three. Trades: you learn the cap by hitting it, and a figure in ink on gold is a new type treatment in the panel.

3. A pile beside the gear coins on edge

The take: the coins stack up on edge in a column at the cluster's left, one per coin carried, with the empty slots as faint lines up to the cap and the count above. It grows and shrinks like a thing you own. Trades: the column moves the gear plate right and adds height at the corner; the pile reads best in motion, when a pickup drops a coin on top.

Tyre gauge icon

1. Four tyres, no kart the footprint

The take: the gauge is about tyres, so the icon is four tyres in the kart's footprint and nothing else: two small at the front, two big at the rear, joined by axles. The whole icon changes colour with the band. Trades: the livery leaves the cluster; it is the most legible at small size.

2. The poster kart side on

The take: the kart as every poster and the Title Scene draw it, side on and facing right, its two octagon wheels toned by the band. One drawing of the kart everywhere. Trades: it is wide, so the gauge takes more of the row; the wheels are a smaller part of the drawing than in the top view.

3. The kart from behind what the chase camera sees

The take: the kart as it sits in front of you all race: rear wing across the top, the two big rear tyres either side, the driver's head above the tub. The tyres are the largest part of this view, so the band shows. Trades: only the rears are visible, and it is the newest drawing of the three.
