# ADR 0007: Art

## Status

Accepted (2026-10-18)

## Context

The previous project died when art direction came before a playable loop.
The fiction is a cartoon parody of Formula 1 with original characters;
nothing may resemble Nintendo characters or circuits.

## Decision

- Placeholder primitives until milestone M1 passes its feel gate ("drift
  feels good" by hand). Then Kenney CC0 car and racing kits.
- The track is authored in Blender and exported as glTF plus a centreline
  spline, checkpoints, item box positions, boost pad positions, the spawn
  grid, and surface tags (asphalt, sand, grass, wall, out of world).
- Blender sources and exported glTF live in `assets/`.

## Consequences

- No art task is scheduled before M1 is accepted.
- The sim consumes the exported track data, never the mesh, so the mesh can
  change without touching gameplay.
- Assets are CC0 so the repo can become public under MIT with CC0 assets.
