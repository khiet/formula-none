Drift spark colours

Each view loops one drift: no sparks before 0.3 s, the first tier from 0.3 s, the second from 0.6 s, release at 1.4 s. Sparks use the emitter's real numbers: 60 per wheel per second, 6 m/s back, 1 to 3 m/s rise, 0.25 s life, 9.8 m/s² gravity, cubes that shrink as they die.

What would change

  • SPARK_COLOURS in apps/web/src/sparks.ts; the rocket start's charge sparks share the table, so the weak start boost turns orange and the full one red.
  • The tier names stay blue and orange in the sim's MiniTurbo and in GLOSSARY unless renamed; a rename touches the sim, the generated types and the replay fixtures' readouts, so it is a separate call.
  • Physics aside: a real spark's colour tracks its temperature, and red is the cooler end (about 700 °C, orange about 1000 °C). Option B leans on that: every spark is born hot and cools to its tier's colour, so red reads as "longer in the fire" by cooling further, not by being hotter.
