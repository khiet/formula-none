Title and Results Variations

Three directions for the Title Scene, each with a Results Scene in the same style. The Results Scenes use only what the race reports today: place, name, best lap and whether the kart finished; "Fastest lap" is the lowest best lap. Pick your finish to see how each one rewards a win, a podium, a midfield finish and not finishing.

Your finish

A. Grand Prix Poster

Arcade cabinet: a crossed-out "1" in the logo, chequered flags rolling, chunky yellow buttons that press in, and the Kenney pixel font from the Instrument panel. Results drop karts onto a podium.

B. Night Circuit

Synthwave night race: neon logo, a grid floor rushing at you, tail-light streaks. The form sits in one glass panel so the world can be loud. Results zoom your place in huge and slide the leaderboard in beside it, with fireworks for a podium.

C. Sticker Book

Sunny toy box: a wobbling sticker logo, candy buttons in red, blue and green, audio toggles as round icon stickers, karts bobbing round a kerbed track. Results hand you a trophy and a rosette in your place's colour, and stamp a fastest lap.
