A third course, Monaco cut

A first draft of a new course after the Monaco street circuit, run clockwise as the real one is: Sainte Devote, the climb up Beau Rivage, Massenet and Casino Square, Mirabeau, the Grand Hotel hairpin, Portier, the tunnel, the chicane, Tabac, the two swimming pool chicanes, La Rascasse and Anthony Noghes. Corner angles are measured from the real circuit and the lap was fitted to its outline at 0.37 scale, with radii opened to kart size. The run-off is a 2 m strip instead of 6 m, so the barriers stand close as they do in the streets. The map is laid live in this page from the same turtle steps as trackgen/layouts, so editing SPEC or PLACEMENTS in the source redraws it.

Reference: the real circuit

3337 m, north up, from the public bacinger/f1-circuits GeoJSON (mc-1929); the red dot is the start line. The draft keeps every corner's direction and roughly its angle. At 1301 m it cannot keep the real proportions everywhere: the pit straight and the harbour are shorter against the corners, because no corner can be tighter than 12 m.

How the draft was made

  1. Split the real outline into 34 pieces at its corners and measured each one's heading change: Sainte Devote turns 84 degrees right, the Grand Hotel hairpin 195 left, La Rascasse 134 right; the whole lap nets 360 right.
  2. Fitted turtle steps to the outline scaled to 0.37 by annealing their lengths, holding each corner's angle, so the lap closes, the grid straight is at least 30 m, and every pair of legs keeps 24 m between centrelines (8 m of grass between barriers with 2 m run-off).
  3. Rounded to whole metres and degrees, opened the hairpin from 13 to 14 m so its two legs keep 24 m, and re-solved Beau Rivage (163.12 m) to close the lap.
  4. Built the layout below through trackgen.layout.build: it closes at 1301.3 m with 12 item boxes, 2 boost pads, 3 kerbs, 15 tyres, 15 checkpoints and 8 grid slots, cresting 8.0 m up at Casino Square.

Elevation

One Hill of 8 m from Sainte Devote to the tunnel mouth, 576 m long, so its crest falls in Casino Square, as the real circuit's high point does. 4.4% at its steepest, a third of the Suzuka bridge's grade, so it reads as a climb and a descent, not a bump. The tunnel, the harbour and the pool sit at sea level.

"""Monaco cut: a clockwise street circuit of about 1301 m after Monaco.

The start/finish straight runs north into Sainte Devote, then the climb up
Beau Rivage to Massenet and Casino Square, down to Mirabeau, round the
Grand Hotel hairpin, Mirabeau Bas and Portier, through the tunnel to the
chicane, along the harbour to Tabac, the two swimming pool chicanes, the
quay, La Rascasse and Anthony Noghes. The run-off is a 2 m strip, so the
barriers stand close as they do in the streets. The road climbs 8 m from
Sainte Devote to its crest at Casino Square and falls again by the tunnel.
"""

from ..layout import (
    BoostPad,
    Decor,
    Grandstands,
    Hoardings,
    ItemRow,
    Kerb,
    Layout,
    TyreLine,
    TyreWall,
    WheelCrossing,
)
from ..path import Hill, Turtle

# Just in from the painted kerb on the inside of a corner.
KERB_INSIDE_M = 4.9
# Left of the centreline, where every tyre wall stands clear of the bots' line.
TYRE_WALL_LEFT_M = 4.5
CREST_HEIGHT_M = 8.0

path = (
    Turtle(x=0.0, y=0.0, heading_deg=98.0)
    .straight("start", 93.0)
    .arc("sainte-devote", 13.0, -84.0)
    .straight("beau-rivage", 163.12)
    .arc("massenet", 37.0, 93.0)
    .straight("casino-approach", 39.0)
    .arc("casino", 26.0, -84.0)
    .straight("casino-descent", 52.0)
    .arc("mirabeau", 14.0, -140.0)
    .arc("mirabeau-kink", 25.0, 27.0)
    .straight("hairpin-approach", 14.0)
    .arc("grand-hotel", 14.0, 195.0)
    .straight("hairpin-exit", 7.0)
    .arc("mirabeau-bas", 13.0, -104.0)
    .straight("portier-approach", 10.0)
    .arc("portier", 13.0, -97.0)
    .straight("tunnel-entry", 34.0)
    .arc("tunnel", 122.0, -81.0)
    .arc("chicane-in", 14.0, 69.0)
    .arc("chicane-out", 12.0, -97.0)
    .arc("chicane-exit", 15.0, 25.0)
    .straight("harbour", 69.0)
    .arc("tabac", 17.0, 54.0)
    .arc("tabac-exit", 100.0, 27.0)
    .arc("pool-1-left", 13.0, 47.0)
    .arc("pool-1-right", 22.0, -45.0)
    .straight("pool-straight", 17.0)
    .arc("pool-2-right", 12.0, -71.0)
    .arc("pool-2-left", 12.0, 73.0)
    .arc("quay", 90.0, 39.0)
    .arc("rascasse", 12.0, -134.0)
    .straight("rascasse-exit", 15.0)
    .arc("noghes", 14.0, -78.0)
    .arc("noghes-flick", 48.0, 27.0)
    .arc("pit-bend", 40.0, -21.0)
    .close("grid")
)

seg = path.segment
# Sainte Devote to the tunnel mouth, so the crest falls in Casino Square.
climb = Hill(seg("sainte-devote").s_start, seg("tunnel-entry").s_end, CREST_HEIGHT_M)

LAYOUT = Layout(
    name="monaco",
    path=path,
    asphalt_half_width=6.0,
    runoff_width=2.0,
    checkpoint_count=15,
    tyre_walls=[
        # Outside Sainte Devote: a right-hander's inside is on the bots' line.
        TyreWall(seg("sainte-devote").s_mid, offset=TYRE_WALL_LEFT_M),
        TyreWall(seg("grand-hotel").s_mid - 8.0, offset=TYRE_WALL_LEFT_M),
        TyreWall(seg("grand-hotel").s_mid + 8.0, offset=TYRE_WALL_LEFT_M),
    ],
    item_rows=[
        ItemRow(seg("beau-rivage").s_mid),
        ItemRow(seg("tunnel-entry").s_mid),
        ItemRow(seg("quay").s_mid),
    ],
    boost_pads=[
        BoostPad(seg("tunnel").s_mid),
        BoostPad(seg("noghes-flick").s_mid, offset=3.0),
    ],
    # Ten on the centreline, down the start straight and the harbour; five
    # off it, wide on the climb and on the pool straight.
    tyre_lines=[
        TyreLine(seg("start").s_start + 10.0, count=5),
        TyreLine(seg("harbour").s_start + 10.0, count=5),
        TyreLine(seg("beau-rivage").s_start + 12.0, count=3, offset=-4.0),
        TyreLine(seg("pool-straight").s_start + 4.0, count=2, offset=4.0),
    ],
    kerbs=[
        Kerb(seg("chicane-out").s_mid, offset=-KERB_INSIDE_M),
        Kerb(seg("pool-1-left").s_mid, offset=KERB_INSIDE_M),
        Kerb(seg("pool-2-right").s_mid, offset=-KERB_INSIDE_M),
    ],
    # Mirabeau turns right, so the descent's outside is the left.
    wheel_crossings=[WheelCrossing(seg("casino-descent").s_mid, outside=1)],
    elevation=[climb],
    decor=Decor(
        hoardings=[
            Hoardings(("casino",), side=-1),
            Hoardings(("portier",), side=1),
            Hoardings(("pool-1-left", "pool-1-right"), side=-1),
            Hoardings(("rascasse",), side=1),
        ],
        grandstands=[
            Grandstands(("grid", "start"), side=-1),
            Grandstands(("harbour",), side=1),
            Grandstands(("pool-straight", "pool-2-right"), side=1),
        ],
        gantry=True,
    ),
)

Engineering notes

  • Choosing the course is its own change. Countdown already carries track: String, so every client builds the right sim once the server names it. What is missing is the creator's pick: a field on Room, a creator-only message to change it, the Room Scene's picker, and an ADR. That belongs in its own issue, ahead of or beside this one.
  • 2 m run-off is new. Both tracks today use 6 m. runoff_width is already per layout, and checkpoints span wall to wall from it, but bots, respawn and the wall bounce were all tuned with 6 m of sand to recover in. A kart that runs wide here meets the barrier almost at once. Worth a scripted lap and a bot sweep before tuning anything.
  • Tight corners. Six corners sit at 12 to 13 m (Sainte Devote, Mirabeau Bas, Portier, the chicane, the second pool chicane, La Rascasse) against the 17 m Suzuka hairpin. The inside barrier is 4 m from the centreline at 12 m. Check with a centreline follower in crates/sim/tests/laps.rs that a kart gets round each without stopping, and that bots do not stall on the hairpin's tyre walls.
  • The tunnel is new scenery. A roof over the road from just past the tunnel mouth to before the chicane, about 150 m. The chase camera rides above and behind the kart, so a low roof would cut through the view; the roof needs to sit above the camera or be drawn only from outside. It is Scenery, so the sidecar does not change. Darker light inside would be the first lighting change along the lap.
  • Spline progress is safe. The course does not cross itself, and the closest legs (the two sides of the hairpin) are 24 m apart, so the windowed search of ADR 0046 has nothing to confuse.
  • Lap length. 1301 m, within 5 m of Bell Deer, so three laps (ADR 0054) take about as long as a race there.
  • Tests and fixtures. A new track adds a sidecar and glTF under assets/tracks/monaco/ from pnpm export:tracks, a case in the layout tests, and probably a replay fixture for the determinism gate. Nothing that exists today has to move.

Decisions

  1. The name in the game?
    1. (Decided) Monarch: Monaco is a principality, and the name keeps its sound.
  2. How wide is the run-off?
    1. (Decided) 2 m, as drafted: the barriers are what makes it Monaco, and it sets the course apart from the other two.
  3. How is the tunnel drawn?
    1. (Decided) Walls and a roof high enough to clear the chase camera, with lights along it.
  4. A jump?
    1. (Decided) None: the crest at Casino Square is the course's lift, and a ramp on a street circuit lands in a barrier.
  5. Which scenery, beyond grandstands and hoardings?
    1. (Decided) The harbour's water and yachts beside the harbour straight and the pool, and the casino building outside Casino Square.
