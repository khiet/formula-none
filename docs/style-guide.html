Foundations

One language, the car and its driver: chibi low-poly for the world, the posters and the Instrument panel (ADR 0034), with a livery as a palette (ADR 0035). Everything below is built from these rules, colours and one face.

World and posters / ADR 0034, 0035, 0036

Chibi low-poly

  • Short wheelbase, oversized wheels, rears bigger than fronts, all inside the sim's 1.2 x 1.8 m box.
  • Flat-shaded facets: every panel in a lit and a shaded tone of one colour. Flat materials, no textures.
  • Wheels and heads are polygons: octagon tyres with three spokes, a faceted head about 0.6 m across.
  • Drivers race bare-headed: big eyes, short jaw, skin and hair swapped on one shared head.
  • The head rolls at the neck against a turn, 8° a g up to 30°, and holds level through a spin-out or a stall: the head sway.
  • A livery is primary, secondary and accent, plus a key colour for every single-colour use.
  • No team name, crest, sponsor or race number on a car.
Instrument panel / ADR 0037

The car's facets, on the View

  • Readouts sit on faceted plates: chamfered corners, a lit, a mid and a shaded band of one colour, and the posters' hard ink drop. No outline.
  • White values, grey labels, a 0.1 em ink drop under each, in the pixel face.
  • Your readouts wear your livery: the position plate in primary, flags and hubs in accent. Other karts show in their key colour.
  • Polygons, not pixels: shift lights are hexagons, map markers octagons, the tyre gauge is your car from above with your driver's hair.
  • The item characters are the View's meshes drawn flat, so the slot and the road show one cone, one drone, one champagne bottle.
  • Sized in HUD units, max(0.55rem, 1.5vmin), so the panel scales with the window and still reads in a small one. Blinks hold steady under reduced motion.

Palette

Colours by role. The Instrument panel's and the items' colours live in apps/web/src/palette.ts, the liveries in apps/web/src/livery.ts; repo-checks fails when this page disagrees with either. A new colour joins a role here before it ships.

Coin

apps/web/src/coin-meshes.ts coinMesh
Size
0.8 m across, 0.12 m thick, its centre 0.6 m up; collected within 1 m of its placement
Decided
ADR 0056, rule by 0055; chosen from top speed pickups

An octagonal prism standing on its edge, a vertex up, lit and flat-shaded: faces in COIN_GOLD #ffd23f, the rim and an F embossed on each face in COIN_RIM #b8860b, the F reading the right way round from either side. It turns once a second on its vertical axis and bobs 5 cm, off the View's clock, so every coin on the track faces the same way at once. Lines of them lie on the straights; a kart that takes one hides it until it is back three seconds later, as a box does, and a bright two-note chime plays.

Don't turn coins on their own phase or lay one flat: a line of coins turning together is what reads as a line to take.

Coin spray

apps/web/src/coin-meshes.ts coinSpray
Timing
0.6 s, half-size coins thrown 3 m/s out and 5 m/s up
Decided
ADR 0056

The spin-out's loss cue: as a kart's count falls, the View throws that many half-size coins out of it, spread evenly round it, spinning as they rise and fall, gone after 0.6 s. Homing projectile

apps/web/src/item-meshes.ts
Size

Decided
ADR 0057, spec in mockups/homing-drone.html

A camera quad, a trackside broadcast FPV drone gone rogue: carbon body and X arms, a red canopy, orange motors, two-blade rotors over translucent blur discs spinning in opposite diagonal pairs, a camera pod whose red lens is its eye locked on, and an antenna whose tally light blinks at 1.5 Hz. Sparks, dust, tyre marks, shadow

apps/web/src/sparks.ts, dust.ts, tell-meshes.ts, exhaust-flame.ts
Decided
ADR 0034

Sparks are box streaks stretched along their flight, dust is a 6 x 4 low-poly sphere, marks are flat quads. The drift tiers run orange then red, each spark born pale and cooling to its tier's colour; red's sparks glow and bounce. The boost's flame and wind lines are additive, so they light the road rather than cover it. The wind lines are the slipstream's tell too (ADR 0077): in another kart's wake they build from nothing to the boost's thinnest rate over the second of the charge, a tow throws them at the boost's full rate and fades them over its last 0.35 s as a boost does, and a boost under way wins; no flame, which stays the boost's.

Under 30% tyre life (ADR 0080), so rivals can read each other: light tyre smoke at the rear wheels while the kart rolls on the ground, #c8c8c8 at 6 puffs a second against a stall's 30, the run-off's dust taking over off the road; and drift marks 1.6 times as dark as a fresh tyre's, fading over the same 4 s.

The shadow disc is a smooth 32-segment circle; its soft edge hides the segments.

Runaway wheel

apps/web/src/item-meshes.ts runawayWheelMesh, runawayWheelFlames; wheel-tells.ts
Size
1.2 m radius (WHEEL_RADIUS_M in tuning.rs), 2.4 m across and 0.96 m wide
Decided
ADR 0041, ADR 0042, ADR 0044, the danger spec

The hot wheel, grown to the sim's reach: an eight-facet tyre in WHEEL_TYRE #26262a, dark so it reads on the grey asphalt, with a red-hot rim on each face, ROCKET_FLAME #ffd23f at the hub to ROCKET.mid #d00a12 at its edge and unlit so it glows, three spokes in ROCKET_STRAP #3a3d42 and a flame-yellow hub. The View rolls it where the sim has it on Bell Deer's run from the bridge to the hairpin: in from one wall, twice off the walls and out through the far one, facing the way it rolls. It turns by the ground it covers and bounces 0.5 m twice each leg, starting over at each wall; a kart it reaches spins out.

Three flame tongues burn off its back, WHEEL_FLAME #ff8800 and see-through round a flame-yellow core, unlit and flickering; they point away from the way it rolls, so they swing round at each wall hit. It leaves grey WHEEL_SMOKE #8a8d94 puffs behind it that rise and fade. Where it lands from each bounce and at each wall hit it kicks up five puffs of the run-off's sand dust #d8c08a that spread and fade over 0.45 s, and at each wall hit eight flame-yellow sparks fly 1.5 m back over the track off the wall where the tyre strikes, fading over 0.3 s. The wall hits and the landings are read from the path the sim reports: a leg starting over, and half way through one.

Through the second the flag is up before a pass it waits, burning, on top of the wall it will roll in from, on the crossing's line, rocking 0.15 rad either way about its axle, and drops to the road as the pass starts. The sim reports the foot of that wall; the View finds the wall's top in the track's glTF. Up there no kart reaches it, and it hits nothing.

It is built in code like the items, not in the track's glTF, because it moves. The bounce is drawn only: the sim rolls it along the ground, so the reach it tests is the one the player sees where the wheel meets the road.

Title Scene

apps/web/src/title-scene.ts, apps/web/src/course-preview.ts, apps/web/src/flags.ts, index.html #title-scene
Decided
ADR 0033, 0061, 0078, 0083

Red poster, rays, chequer bands top and bottom, the logo, chunky buttons and fields with hard ink shadows, and the audio toggles as ink chips: a lit square when on, the label struck through when off. Poster karts in their liveries run along the bottom edge, wheels turning.

Two columns: the way in on the left, the course on the right. The course is a heading, a flag tile per course in the registry in track.ts (a radio group named course) and a preview card. The picked tile turns poster gold and lifts on a hard ink shadow; the others dim their flag. The card names the course in gold over "After" its circuit, draws the sidecar's centreline flat in asphalt over a white wall with the start line and a gold dot lapping it every eight seconds, still under reduced motion, then a blurb and four numbers measured from the centreline: lap, laps, tightest bend and longest straight.

Under the flags, the bots: a "Bots" heading and a tile per level, Basic, Pro and Ace (a radio group named bots), the flag tile with its caption in the flag's place, picked gold and lifted, the arrows moving between them. One line on an ink plate describes the picked level in the blurb's voice: "Ace: corner flat out, fire on sight, never wait for you." One pick covers Create room and Solo race, and it is remembered beside the course pick (ADR 0078).

Beside the bots, the Tyres pick, drawn the same way: a "Tyres" heading, tiles Off and On (a radio group named tyres) and one line on an ink plate, "On: driving wears them." On is the default; one pick covers Create room and Solo race, and it is remembered beside the course pick (ADR 0083).

Beside the Tyres, the laps, drawn the same way without the line: a "Laps" heading and tiles 3 and 5 (a radio group named laps). 3 is the default; one pick covers Create room and Solo race, it is remembered beside the course pick, and the card's Laps number follows it (ADR 0084).

The flags are the real ones in their own colours: Japan's at 3:2, and the Union Flag and Monaco's red over white cropped from 2:1 and 5:4 to the tile's 3:2. Monaco's red is FLAG_MC_RED #ce1126.

Go green marks the one primary action on a poster; poster gold marks the others.

Why a create or a join came back to the Title reads as a line of text in the way-in column: "The server is full; try again later" past the room cap, "Too many new rooms from here; try again shortly" past the address's limit (ADR 0073).

Poster kart: Title runners and the podium

apps/web/src/poster.ts
Decided
ADR 0033, 0035, 0036, 0051, 0052

The kart side on, facing right, drawn from docs/kart-side.js: every face of kart.glb that shows from the kart's right, pitched down four degrees so each top face is a lit strip and the far wheels a sliver of tyre, shaded by its normal against one light in three tones of its material's colour, in painter order. Primary, secondary and accent as the View paints them, the carbon halo and pillar, the octagon wheels with their three spokes, the bare head in only the driver's parts. Nothing here is traced by hand: pnpm generate:poster rewrites it from the glb, and CI fails on a stale copy.
