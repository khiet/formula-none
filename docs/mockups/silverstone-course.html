Another course, Silverstone cut

A first draft of a new course after Silverstone, run clockwise as the real one is, from the grid on the Hamilton Straight: Abbey, Farm, Village, The Loop and Aintree, the Wellington Straight, Brooklands and Luffield, Woodcote and the National Straight, Copse, the Maggotts and Becketts esses, Chapel, the Hangar Straight, Stowe, Vale and Club. Corner angles are measured from the real circuit and the lap was fitted to its outline at 0.22 scale, with radii opened to kart size. Where the Monaco draft is slow and walled in, this one is flat, open and fast, with the standard 6 m run-off. The map is laid live in this page from the same turtle steps as trackgen/layouts, so editing SPEC or PLACEMENTS in the source redraws it.

Reference: the real circuit

5891 m, north up, from the public bacinger/f1-circuits GeoJSON (gb-1948); the red dot is the start line on the Hamilton Straight. The draft keeps every corner's direction and roughly its angle. At 1323 m the straights keep their order of length (Hangar, then Wellington, then National) but are shorter against the corners, because the esses cannot shrink below kart radii.

How the draft was made

  1. Split the real outline into 27 pieces at its corners and measured each one's heading change: Copse turns about 66 degrees right, Becketts 95 right, Luffield 193 right, The Loop 134 left; the whole lap nets 360 right.
  2. Fitted turtle steps to the outline scaled to 0.22 by annealing their lengths, holding each corner's angle, so the lap closes, the grid straight is at least 45 m, and every pair of legs keeps 32 m between centrelines (8 m of grass between walls with 6 m run-off), as on Bell Deer.
  3. Rounded to whole metres and degrees and re-solved the Hangar Straight (175.74 m) to close the lap.
  4. Built the layout below through trackgen.layout.build: it closes at 1323.4 m with 12 item boxes, 2 boost pads, 5 kerbs, 11 tyres, 15 checkpoints and 8 grid slots. No elevation: the real circuit is an old airfield.

Layout draft

Drop-in shape for assets/tracks/trackgen/layouts/silverstone.py, in the style of bell_deer.py. It builds and closes as written; the map above draws from the same numbers. Woods and the hangar props are left out; the scenery is settled below (question 3) and comes with the build.

"""Silverstone cut: a flat, fast, clockwise circuit of about 1323 m after Silverstone.

The grid sits on the Hamilton Straight by the pits. The lap runs through
Abbey and Farm to Village, The Loop and Aintree, down the Wellington Straight
to Brooklands and the long right of Luffield, past Woodcote onto the National
Straight, then Copse, the Maggotts and Becketts esses and Chapel, the Hangar
Straight to Stowe, and Vale and Club back onto the grid. The ground is an old
airfield, so there is no elevation; the lap is about speed and the esses.
"""

from ..layout import (
    BoostPad,
    Decor,
    Grandstands,
    Hoardings,
    ItemRow,
    Kerb,
    Layout,
    Prop,
    Spot,
    TyreLine,
    TyreWall,
    WheelCrossing,
)
from ..path import Turtle

# Just in from the painted kerb on the inside of a corner.
KERB_INSIDE_M = 4.9
# Left of the centreline, where every tyre wall stands clear of the bots' line.
TYRE_WALL_LEFT_M = 4.5

path = (
    Turtle(x=0.0, y=0.0, heading_deg=52.5)
    .straight("start", 29.0)
    .arc("abbey", 25.0, -57.0)
    .straight("abbey-exit", 13.0)
    .arc("farm", 33.0, 39.0)
    .straight("village-approach", 31.0)
    .arc("village", 15.0, -103.0)
    .arc("the-loop", 12.0, 134.0)
    .arc("aintree", 39.0, 64.0)
    .straight("wellington", 145.0)
    .arc("brooklands", 17.0, 113.0)
    .straight("brooklands-exit", 10.0)
    .arc("luffield", 18.0, -193.0)
    .straight("woodcote-approach", 18.0)
    .arc("woodcote", 47.0, -54.0)
    .straight("national", 82.0)
    .arc("copse", 22.0, -66.0)
    .straight("copse-exit", 80.0)
    .arc("maggotts", 47.0, 22.0)
    .arc("becketts-right", 27.0, -46.0)
    .arc("becketts-left", 20.0, 56.0)
    .arc("becketts", 24.0, -95.0)
    .arc("chapel", 39.0, 31.0)
    .straight("hangar", 175.74)
    .arc("stowe", 37.0, -125.0)
    .straight("vale-approach", 102.0)
    .arc("vale", 15.0, 72.0)
    .arc("club", 20.0, -152.0)
    .close("grid")
)

seg = path.segment

LAYOUT = Layout(
    name="silverstone",
    path=path,
    asphalt_half_width=6.0,
    runoff_width=6.0,
    checkpoint_count=15,
    tyre_walls=[
        # Outside the right-handers: their inside is on the bots' line.
        TyreWall(seg("luffield").s_mid - 10.0, offset=TYRE_WALL_LEFT_M),
        TyreWall(seg("luffield").s_mid + 10.0, offset=TYRE_WALL_LEFT_M),
        TyreWall(seg("stowe").s_mid, offset=TYRE_WALL_LEFT_M),
    ],
    item_rows=[
        ItemRow(seg("wellington").s_mid),
        ItemRow(seg("national").s_mid),
        ItemRow(seg("copse-exit").s_mid),
    ],
    boost_pads=[
        BoostPad(seg("abbey-exit").s_mid, offset=3.0),
        BoostPad(seg("hangar").s_start + 40.0),
    ],
    # Five on the centreline off the grid; six off it, wide on the Wellington
    # and Hangar Straights.
    tyre_lines=[
        TyreLine(seg("start").s_start + 10.0, count=5),
        TyreLine(seg("wellington").s_start + 12.0, count=3, offset=4.0),
        TyreLine(seg("hangar").s_mid + 30.0, count=3, offset=-4.0),
    ],
    kerbs=[
        Kerb(seg("the-loop").s_mid, offset=KERB_INSIDE_M),
        Kerb(seg("becketts-right").s_mid, offset=-KERB_INSIDE_M),
        Kerb(seg("becketts-left").s_mid, offset=KERB_INSIDE_M),
        Kerb(seg("becketts").s_mid, offset=-KERB_INSIDE_M),
        Kerb(seg("vale").s_mid, offset=KERB_INSIDE_M),
    ],
    # Vale turns left, so the approach's outside is the right.
    wheel_crossings=[WheelCrossing(seg("vale-approach").s_mid, outside=-1)],
    elevation=[],
    decor=Decor(
        hoardings=[
            Hoardings(("luffield",), side=1),
            Hoardings(("copse",), side=1),
            Hoardings(("becketts",), side=1),
            Hoardings(("stowe",), side=1),
        ],
        grandstands=[
            Grandstands(("grid", "start"), side=1),
            Grandstands(("luffield",), side=-1),
            Grandstands(("copse-exit",), side=-1),
            Grandstands(("vale-approach",), side=1),
        ],
        gantry=True,
        props=[
            Prop("pits", Spot(s=0.0, offset=-26.0), length=70.0),
        ],
    ),
)

Engineering notes

  • Choosing the course is its own change. As in the Monaco draft: Countdown already carries track: String, but the creator's pick needs a field on Room, a creator-only message, the Room Scene's picker and an ADR. One issue covers both new courses.
  • Nothing new to build. Every piece here exists on Bell Deer: 6 m run-off, tyre walls, kerbs, the wheel crossing, grandstands, hoardings, the gantry and the pits prop. No elevation, no bridge, no new scenery kind. Of the two drafts this is the cheaper one to land first.
  • Fast corners need checking at kart speed. Copse, Abbey, Maggotts and Chapel are 22 to 47 m: flat out in a kart, as they are in the real car. The esses only work if a kart has to lift for Becketts (24 m, 95 degrees). A centreline follower in crates/sim/tests/laps.rs should show a lift there and none through Maggotts; if Becketts is flat too, tighten it to 20 m.
  • The Loop is the slowest corner. 12 m and 134 degrees straight after Village (15 m, 103 degrees right): a right-left at the tightest radius on any course. Check that bots get through without stalling on the kerb.
  • Spline progress is safe. The course does not cross itself, and the closest legs are 32 m apart, so the windowed search of ADR 0046 has nothing to confuse.
  • Lap length. 1323 m, within 20 m of Bell Deer and the Monaco draft, so three laps (ADR 0054) take about as long as a race there; faster corners make it a few seconds quicker.
  • Tests and fixtures. A new track adds a sidecar and glTF under assets/tracks/silverstone/ from pnpm export:tracks, a case in the layout tests, and probably a replay fixture for the determinism gate.

Decisions

  1. The name in the game?
    1. (Decided) Goldstone: Silverstone with the metal swapped.
  2. Elevation?
    1. (Decided) None, as drafted: flat is true to the airfield, and it sets the course apart from Monaco's climb and the Bell Deer bridge.
  3. Which scenery, beyond grandstands, hoardings and the pits?
    1. (Decided) A row of airfield hangars outside the Hangar Straight, and the pits drawn long and white as a nod to the Wing.
  4. A jump?
    1. (Decided) None: the course's character is speed through the esses, and a ramp on a flat airfield has nothing to explain it.
  5. Which new course lands first?
    1. (Decided) Silverstone: it uses only pieces that exist, so it proves the course picker without new tuning.
