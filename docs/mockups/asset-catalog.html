Formula None / asset review, 2026-10-26

Asset Catalog

Every graphic the game draws, checked against the base design and redrawn in it. The base is one language, the car and its driver: chibi low-poly for the world, the posters and the Instrument panel (ADR 0034, with drivers bare-headed per ADR 0036 and liveries as palettes per ADR 0035). The panel still ships the 16-bit kart racer of ADR 0023 and 0024; this page redraws it from the car, as ADR 0037 decides. Where the base mockup and a later ADR disagree, the ADR wins.

Livery
World and posters / ADR 0034, 0035, 0036

Chibi low-poly

  • Short wheelbase, oversized wheels, rears bigger than fronts, all inside the sim's 1.2 x 1.8 m box.
  • Flat-shaded facets: every panel in a lit and a shaded tone of one colour. Flat materials, no textures.
  • Wheels and heads are polygons: octagon tyres with three spokes, a faceted head about 0.6 m across.
  • Drivers race bare-headed: big eyes, short jaw, skin and hair swapped on one shared head.
  • A livery is primary, secondary and accent, plus a key colour for every single-colour use.
  • No team name, crest, sponsor or race number on a car.
Instrument panel / ADR 0037, superseding the look of 0023, 0024

The car's facets, on the View

  • Readouts sit on faceted plates: chamfered corners, a lit, a mid and a shaded band of one colour, and the posters' hard ink drop. No outline.
  • Kenney Mini Square stays, as on the posters: white values, grey labels, a 0.1 em ink drop. The brown outline and the HUD gold retire.
  • Your readouts wear your livery: the position plate in primary, flags and hubs in accent. Other karts show in their key colour.
  • Polygons, not pixels: shift lights are hexagons, map markers octagons, the tyre gauge is your car from above with your driver's hair.
  • The item characters are the View's meshes drawn flat, so the slot and the road show one cone, one gull, one snail.
  • Kept from ADR 0023 and 0031: the time notation, a flag per lap, the HUD unit, steady blinks under reduced motion, the greybox line.

The world

What the View draws in Three.js: the car, the drivers, the item meshes, the tells, and the tracks with their walls, tyre walls, pads and scenery.

Aligned

The car

assets/karts/kart.glb, built by assets/karts/build.py

Built from primitives: tub, sidepods, wings, halo at chin height, octagon wheels with three spokes, one flat material per palette colour. This drawing is the base mockup with ADR 0035 and 0036 applied.

The base mockup still shows a helmet, the race number 29, FORMULA NONE on the rear wing and a red tyre stripe. The ADRs removed all four and the build has none of them, so the catalog drops them too.

The drivers

apps/web/src/livery.ts, hair-* and facial-hair-* parts in karts/build.py

One shared faceted head, fixed whites and irises, hair and facial hair in one colour, earrings in fixed gold. This is the portrait the Room and Results Scenes will use when that ticket lands.

The base mockup gave each driver an iris colour; ADR 0036 fixes them, so every portrait here uses the build's one iris.

Aligned

Sparks, dust, tyre marks, shadow

sparks.ts, dust.ts, tell-meshes.ts

Sparks are box streaks stretched along their flight, dust is a 6 x 4 low-poly sphere, marks are flat quads: already faceted. The drift tiers run orange then red, each spark born pale and cooling to its tier's colour; red's sparks glow and bounce. The boost's flame and wind lines are additive, so they light the road rather than cover it.

One small note: the shadow disc is a smooth 32-segment circle. Its soft edge hides the segments, so it can stay.

Aligned

Track-limit walls

assets/tracks/trackgen/layout.py:243, mesh.py:33

A box rail in the wall white along both edges of the run-off, the whole way round every track (ADR 0022). Flat faces, so the sun alone gives the lit top and the shaded face; the course map draws the same white edge.

Plain white with no stripe or tyre stack. It reads against sand and grass, and a pattern would only add faces along hundreds of metres.

Aligned

Tyre wall

assets/tracks/trackgen/layout.py tyre_stacks, mesh.py drum

A sixteen-sided prism in tyre shade #121214, tagged tyres, with two rings of five eight-sided tyres standing on their treads over it, 0.6 m across, faces out in #2e2e32. The upper ring turns half a tyre from the lower, and each is belted round its middle in kerb red and white. It stands on the asphalt where the pipes stood, into the oval's turns and either side of the Brands Patch hairpins; a kart bounces off it (ADR 0041).

The 16-bit key's tyre barrier, original (ADR 0007): black rubber in rings, with the kerb's red and white so it reads as track furniture against the asphalt. The tyres are scenery; only the prism collides, so the picture can change without touching a rule.

Aligned

Sausage kerb

assets/tracks/trackgen/layout.py sausage_kerbs

A capsule in gold #ffd23f over the sim's 8 x 1 m rectangle, its ends rounded inside it, standing 0.15 m proud as a squashed half-round, with six black #111111 stripes across it. It floats on the asphalt as the other scenery does, just in from the painted kerb on the inside of the Brands Patch chicane; a kart on the ground that reaches it is thrown into a hop that lands straight, and loses its drift (ADR 0041).

The 16-bit key's raised kerb, original (ADR 0007): the yellow of a marshal's flag, so it reads as a warning, striped so its length reads at speed. It is scenery; the launch is the sim's rectangle, so the picture can change without touching a rule.

Boost pad

assets/tracks/build.py:213, crates/sim/src/tuning.rs:168

The export writes each pad as an empty in the BoostPads collection and the sim boosts any kart on its 6 x 4 m rectangle, but nothing gives it a mesh. Players hear boost-pad.wav and feel the boost on a stretch of plain asphalt.

Fix: a flat carbon plate the size of the sim's rectangle, a hair above the asphalt like the kerbs, with three chevrons pointing along the track in the mini-turbo orange, so the pad and the boost it gives share a colour.

The Instrument panel

Now is what ships: the 16-bit kart racer of ADR 0023 and 0024, rules copied from apps/web/index.html. Its brown outline, gold type and pixel sprites belong to a different game from the faceted car and its driver. Aligned redraws each piece from the car: shaded plates, polygons for pixels, your livery on your own readouts. Both at a HUD unit of 9 px over a mock Race View; pick a livery above to repaint yours.

Drift

Position, laps, times

instrument-panel.ts, format.ts

Gold pixel type in a brown outline: right for a 16-bit game, but nothing on the car is outlined and nothing in the world is that gold.

Fix: the position on a plate in your livery's primary, shaded like the car's nose; laps, time and best on carbon plates, the colour of the car's floor. Flags fold like a low-poly flag, chequered once done. The notation stays.

Drift

Item slot and its characters

apps/web/src/item-sprites.ts

16 x 16 sprites in the panel's pixel language, not the world's: the cone in the slot and the cone on the road are two different cones, and the gull in the slot meets a red ball on the road.

Fix: the slot is the item box seen face on, in its golds; the characters are the View's meshes from the world cards drawn flat: the faceted cone, the low-poly gull, and a snail with a faceted shell and a red rocket. Firing lights the plate. The item's name stays as the slot's text.
