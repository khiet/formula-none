Track obstacles spec

The pipes go. In their place: tyre walls, a sausage kerb and a runaway wheel, the last of which moves. All three run in the shared sim, so the server, the client's prediction and the replay fixtures agree, and none of them adds a byte to a snapshot.

Tyre wall

StaticReplaces the pipe

The rule

A stack of tyres with the pipe's footprint. A kart that hits it bounces away the way it would bounce off a kart standing still: a bump straight out from the stack, the least bump plus the tuned share of the closing speed, and a loss of the tuned share of that speed, scaled by how square it hit. A glancing touch keeps nearly all the kart's speed; a square hit sends it back the way it came, slower. It never spins a kart out.

A wall still holds a kart with no bounce; only a tyre wall bounces. A kart held against both gets both: the wall's hold, then the tyre wall's bump with the part into the wall taken off.

Numbers (tuning.rs)

ConstantValueMeaning
TYRE_WALL_RESTITUTION0.4Share of the closing speed the bump carries away, on top of KART_BUMP_MIN_M_PER_S (2.0). A kart has to share a touch with another; a tyre wall gives the kart its whole share.
TYRE_WALL_SPEED_LOSS0.7Share of the closing speed lost on a square hit. At top speed (30 m/s) head on: 9 m/s left, a 14 m/s bump back.
Decay0.3 sUnchanged: KART_BUMP_DECAY_S.

Starting values, settled in a feel check on hairpin 1.

Changes

  • Surface: a fifth tag, tyres, in trackgen/layout.py, Surface and its code. It does not slow karts (nothing drives on it), and surface_at answers it like a wall from above.
  • Layout: Pipe becomes TyreWall(s, offset). The collision mesh is a 16-sided prism, radius 1.5 m, 1.2 m tall, tagged tyres; the tyres themselves are scenery stacked over it: two rings of five tyres, 0.6 m across, each ring with a red and white belt.
  • Sim: after the solver, beside bump_off_karts, a kart in contact with a tyres collider is bumped along the contact normal by Handling::bumped, judged by the velocity it was sent off with, as kart contact is. bumped takes the restitution and loss as arguments; kart contact passes the kart constants. held_by_walls already skips anything not tagged wall.
  • Prediction: nothing new; the tyre walls are in the static track it steps on, so a bounce is predicted.
  • Bots: nothing; the walls stand where the pipes did, left of the bots' line.

Tests

  • Replaces a_kart_driving_into_a_pipe_stops_at_it: a kart driven from oval slot 0 head on into the turn 1 tyre wall never passes through it, and within a second of the touch is moving away from it.
  • A kart that meets it square leaves slower than one that meets it at a slant, and both keep moving.
  • A tyre wall touch never spins a kart out and raises no event.
  • walls_and_pipes_read_as_wall_from_above becomes: the walls read as wall and the tyre walls as tyres from above.
  • Layout check (pnpm test:tracks): every tyre wall stands on the asphalt, clear of the bots' line.

Sausage kerb

Static

The rule

A raised yellow kerb on the inside of the chicane's apex. A kart on the ground whose centre crosses onto it is launched: a forced hop of the hop's airtime, which lands straight. The launch costs the tuned share of the kart's speed, and a drift under way ends there with its charge gone: no mini-turbo on landing, no sparks in the air.

The kerb fires once, as the kart reaches it, like a boost pad. A kart already in the air passes over it untouched, and a kart sitting on it is not launched again until it has left it.

Numbers (tuning.rs)

ConstantValueMeaning
KERB_HALF_LENGTH_M4.0Along the track; the layout's scenery mirrors it, held by the track tests as boost pads are.
KERB_HALF_WIDTH_M0.5Across the track.
KERB_SPEED_KEEP0.85Share of the kart's speed left after the launch.
Airtime0.3 sHOP_AIRTIME_S, launched at the hop's speed.

Changes

  • Sidecar: a kerbs list of placements beside boostPads: centre, heading, from a Kerbs collection of empties. Track::on_a_kerb(point) mirrors on_a_boost_pad.
  • Layout: Kerb(s, offset) on Layout.kerbs; the scenery is a yellow capsule with black stripes, 0.15 m proud, floating on the asphalt as scenery does, so the sim's surface is unchanged.
  • Sim: Situation gains kerb: bool. In Handling::tick, a grounded kart entering a kerb sets the hop's airtime and launch speed, clears drift and hop_direction, and scales its speed by the keep. on_kerb sits in the hash next to on_boost_pad.
  • Prediction and client: nothing new; the launch is the handling's, so the prediction and the drawn hop already follow it.
  • Bots: their line clears it by 1.3 m. A bot test holds them to it; if pursuit steering cuts the apex, the bot's line point steers away from a kerb inside the lookahead.

Tests

  • A kart driven onto the kerb leaves the ground for the hop's airtime and lands with the kept share of its speed.
  • A kart drifting across the kerb lands with no drift and gets no mini-turbo, however long it had charged.
  • A kart that hopped before the kerb and is in the air over it is not launched and keeps its drift.
  • A kart driving beside the kerb is untouched.
  • Eight bots racing three laps of Brands Patch are never launched by the kerb.

Runaway wheel

Moving

The rule

A loose wheel rolls across the approach to hairpin 2, from wall to wall, on a fixed cycle that starts at Go. Each pass goes the other way from the last. A kart it reaches spins out, the same as a hazard hit, and the wheel rolls on: it is never used up, but it hits a kart once a pass. A kart already spun out is not hit again.

A marshal on the outside waves a yellow flag from a second before each pass until it ends. The wheel and the flag are both a pure function of the race clock, so every screen sees the same pass at the same moment.

The cycle

The first pass starts WHEEL_FIRST_PASS_S after Go, so the leader's first lap meets a quiet approach.

Numbers (tuning.rs)

ConstantValueMeaning
WHEEL_PERIOD_S7.0From one pass's start to the next.
WHEEL_PASS_S1.6Wall to wall: 24 m at 15 m/s.
WHEEL_WARNING_S1.0The flag is up this long before each pass, and through it.
WHEEL_FIRST_PASS_S20.0Race clock at the first pass's start.
WHEEL_RADIUS_M0.45Reach, from above, against the kart's collision box, as HAZARD_RADIUS_M is.

Changes

  • Sidecar: a wheelCrossings list of placements from a WheelCrossings collection: the crossing's centre, its heading along the track and its half_width, wall to wall. The timing stays in tuning.rs (ADR 0014).
  • Layout: WheelCrossing(s) on Layout.wheel_crossings, and its marshal post as scenery on the outside run-off: a white box with an orange lamp.
  • Sim: pub fn wheel(track, crossing, elapsed_s) -> Wheel, where Wheel is Quiet, Warning or Rolling { position }. In step, while Racing and after the hazards, each rolling wheel spins out every kart it reaches that is not spun out and has not been hit this pass. A per-kart "last pass hit" number sits in the state and the hash.
  • Events: RaceEvent::SpunOut { kart, by: Option<usize> }, with None for the wheel. The protocol's Hit carries by: Option<u8>; regenerate the TypeScript types and fixtures.
  • Prediction: its race keeps the race clock, so it runs the wheel too: a hit is predicted at once instead of waiting a snapshot, unlike a hazard's. The snapshot's spin-out timer stays the word on it.
  • Bridge and View: the bridge exposes wheel. The Race View draws the wheel (a tyre and hub from the kart's wheel, bouncing three times a pass, the bounce drawn only) and the waving flag from the prediction's race clock, so the wheel a player sees is the one that hits their kart.
  • Bots: a bot within one pass's travel of a crossing lifts off the throttle while wheel says the wheel will cross its line before it gets there, and drives on once it has passed.

Tests

  • A kart parked on the crossing, on the wheel's path, spins out as the first pass reaches it, and the event names no kart.
  • The same kart parked beside the crossing is never hit through three passes.
  • A kart standing on the path is hit once a pass, not every tick of it.
  • Through the countdown and the outro the wheel is quiet and hits nothing.
  • wheel is Warning from WHEEL_WARNING_S before each pass and Rolling through it, and alternates direction.
  • A prediction run beside the server's race reports the wheel's spin-out on the same tick.
  • Eight bots racing three laps of Brands Patch are never hit by the wheel.
  • Protocol: a Hit with no by round-trips.

Shared changes

Determinism and replays

  • Nothing here reads a clock outside the sim: the wheel reads the race clock, which the state already hashes.
  • Every fixture under assets/replays is re-recorded: the tyre walls change collisions on both tracks, and the new fields change the hash.
  • A new fixture, brands-patch-chicane.replay.json, drives across the kerb and through a wheel pass, so the replay job checks both rules natively and in wasm.

Network

  • No snapshot field is added: each kart's spin-out timer already carries a wheel hit, and the wheel is computed from the clock.
  • The one protocol change is Hit's optional by.

Docs

  • ADR 0041, Track obstacles: tyre walls replace pipes; the kerb and the wheel; the wheel's spin-out is by no one; obstacles that move are a function of the race clock, never in the snapshot.
  • GLOSSARY: add tyre wall, sausage kerb, runaway wheel; surface gains tyres; spin-out says "by an item or the runaway wheel"; bump notes the tyre wall gives one.
  • Asset catalog: cards for the tyre stack, the kerb, the wheel and the marshal post, in the 16-bit key, original (ADR 0007).
  • Track README: the new tag, collections and layout fields.
