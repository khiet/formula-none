Sky Objects

Monarch and Goldstone fly nothing over their roads, while Bell Deer has its airship. Each card below is one flying prop that could give those two skies the same kind of joke. Pick one per course. The moving preview is the Race View's sky over each course's own landmark (the harbour and casino, the hangars and pits). The still below it is the prop side on, nose to the right, drawn the way the style guide draws the airship.

Every option keeps the airship's rules from ADR 0050 and scenery-motion.ts: it is scenery on the View's own clock, the sim never sees it, it has no shadow and no sound, its lowest point stays 30 m over every road, and it only wears sponsors already in sponsors.py.

Monarch

The harbour course. Its sky sits over the harbour water and the casino's copper dome.

A. Seaplane

A white flying boat with a red parasol wing that circles the harbour as if it is about to land among the yachts.

Size
14 m long, 15 m span
Flies
Once round the harbour every 3 min at 38 m, banked 10° into the turn
Moves
Propeller disc spins
Wears
DIRTY AIR CONDITIONING on the hull, ink-blue
Build
A hull swept like the airship's, plus wing, fin and nacelle. Same circle motion with a bank added.

B. Helicopter

Recommended

A white helicopter on a slow orbit between the casino and the harbour mouth, its rotor always turning. It is the only option that moves up close and not just across the sky.

Size
12.8 m long, 11 m rotor
Flies
Once round every 2 min at 35 m, nose dipped 5°
Moves
Main and tail rotors turn, the same kind of motion as the Circuit Wheel's rim
Wears
LATE BRAKING BANK on the cabin, ink-purple
Build
The smallest mesh. Two rotor nodes for scenery-motion.ts to turn, found by name like the wheel's rim.

C. Hot-air balloon

A red-and-white striped balloon drifting over the harbour, bobbing slowly, with its burner flickering under the envelope.

Size
16 m across, 26 m from basket to crown
Flies
Once round every 6 min at 40 m, bobbing 2 m on a 12 s swell
Moves
Bob only; the burner flame is a lamp cone that blinks
Wears
CHICANE COLA on a white belt, ink-red
Build
Twelve gores swept from a profile, the same method as the airship's hull. No turning parts.

Goldstone

The old airfield course. Its sky sits over the hangars by the Hangar Straight and the white pits.

A. Banner biplane

Recommended

A yellow biplane towing a sponsor banner over the hangars it might have flown out of. Its banner is the most readable lettering of all six options.

Size
8 m plane, 12 m rope, 22 by 3.4 m banner
Flies
Once round every 3 min at 36 m (the banner's foot at 32 m)
Moves
Propeller disc spins
Wears
OVERSTEER INSURANCE on the banner, ink-blue, 2 m type
Build
One node with the banner as its child, flown on the airship's circle. Needs kerb-yellow, which is already a material.

B. Display jets

Three red display jets in an arrowhead, each trailing a smoke ribbon. A fast flypast every lap or so, not a slow drifter.

Size
9 m jets, 50 m smoke ribbons
Flies
Once round a wide circle every 45 s at 60 m
Moves
The whole formation; the smoke is fixed ribbons that ride with it, not particles
Wears
No lettering (it is too small at 60 m); kerb-red with a wall flash
Build
Three small hulls plus ribbons. Moves fastest, so it is the easiest to miss and the busiest on screen.

C. Rain cloud

A joke about the weather at the real circuit: one low-poly grey cloud that wanders round the course raining on nobody in particular.

Size
30 m across, rain streaks 12 m long
Flies
Once round every 5 min at 45 m (the streaks end at 33 m)
Moves
The streaks scroll down through a loop, which needs a new kind of motion
Wears
Nothing
Build
Puffs of beam-top and stand-riser over a flat beam-shade base. It must not dim or wet the road: the rain is decoration, not weather.
