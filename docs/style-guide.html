Foundations

One language, the car and its driver: chibi low-poly for the world, the posters and the Instrument panel (ADR 0034), with a livery as a palette (ADR 0035). Everything below is built from these rules, colours and one face.

World and posters / ADR 0034, 0035, 0036

Chibi low-poly

  • Short wheelbase, oversized wheels, rears bigger than fronts, all inside the sim's 1.2 x 1.8 m box.
  • Flat-shaded facets: every panel in a lit and a shaded tone of one colour. Flat materials, no textures.
  • Wheels and heads are polygons: octagon tyres with three spokes, a faceted head about 0.6 m across.
  • Drivers race bare-headed: big eyes, short jaw, skin and hair swapped on one shared head.
  • The head rolls at the neck against a turn, 8° a g up to 30°, and holds level through a spin-out or a stall: the head sway.
  • A livery is primary, secondary and accent, plus a key colour for every single-colour use.
  • No team name, crest, sponsor or race number on a car.
Instrument panel / ADR 0037

The car's facets, on the View

  • Readouts sit on faceted plates: chamfered corners, a lit, a mid and a shaded band of one colour, and the posters' hard ink drop. No outline.
  • White values, grey labels, a 0.1 em ink drop under each, in the pixel face.
  • Your readouts wear your livery: the position plate in primary, flags and hubs in accent. Other karts show in their key colour.
  • Polygons, not pixels: shift lights are hexagons, map markers octagons, the tyre gauge is your car from above with your driver's hair.
  • The item characters are the View's meshes drawn flat, so the slot and the road show one cone, one drone, one champagne bottle.
  • Sized in HUD units, max(0.55rem, 1.5vmin), so the panel scales with the window and still reads in a small one. Blinks hold steady under reduced motion.

Palette

Colours by role. The Instrument panel's and the items' colours live in apps/web/src/palette.ts, the liveries in apps/web/src/livery.ts; repo-checks fails when this page disagrees with either. A new colour joins a role here before it ships.

Coin

apps/web/src/coin-meshes.ts coinMesh
Size
0.8 m across, 0.12 m thick, its centre 0.6 m up; collected within 1 m of its placement
Decided
ADR 0056, rule by 0055; chosen from top speed pickups

An octagonal prism standing on its edge, a vertex up, lit and flat-shaded: faces in COIN_GOLD #ffd23f, the rim and an F embossed on each face in COIN_RIM #b8860b, the F reading the right way round from either side. It turns once a second on its vertical axis and bobs 5 cm, off the View's clock, so every coin on the track faces the same way at once. Lines of them lie on the straights; a kart that takes one hides it until it is back three seconds later, as a box does, and a bright two-note chime plays.

Don't turn coins on their own phase or lay one flat: a line of coins turning together is what reads as a line to take.

Coin spray

apps/web/src/coin-meshes.ts coinSpray
Timing
0.6 s, half-size coins thrown 3 m/s out and 5 m/s up
Decided
ADR 0056

The spin-out's loss cue: as a kart's count falls, the View throws that many half-size coins out of it, spread evenly round it, spinning as they rise and fall, gone after 0.6 s. Homing projectile

Size

Decided
ADR 0057, spec in mockups/homing-drone.html

A camera quad, a trackside broadcast FPV drone gone rogue: carbon body and X arms, a red canopy, orange motors, two-blade rotors over translucent blur discs spinning in opposite diagonal pairs, a camera pod whose red lens is its eye locked on, and an antenna whose tally light blinks at 1.5 Hz. Runaway wheel

apps/web/src/item-meshes.ts runawayWheelMesh, runawayWheelFlames; wheel-tells.ts
Size
1.2 m radius (WHEEL_RADIUS_M in tuning.rs), 2.4 m across and 0.96 m wide
Decided
ADR 0041, ADR 0042, ADR 0044, the danger spec

The hot wheel, grown to the sim's reach: an eight-facet tyre in WHEEL_TYRE #26262a, dark so it reads on the grey asphalt, with a red-hot rim on each face, ROCKET_FLAME #ffd23f at the hub to ROCKET.mid #d00a12 at its edge and unlit so it glows, three spokes in ROCKET_STRAP #3a3d42 and a flame-yellow hub. The View rolls it where the sim has it on Suzuqa Patch's run from the bridge to the hairpin: in from one wall, twice off the walls and out through the far one, facing the way it rolls. It turns by the ground it covers and bounces 0.5 m twice each leg, starting over at each wall; a kart it reaches spins out.

Three flame tongues burn off its back, WHEEL_FLAME #ff8800 and see-through round a flame-yellow core, unlit and flickering; they point away from the way it rolls, so they swing round at each wall hit. It leaves grey WHEEL_SMOKE #8a8d94 puffs behind it that rise and fade. Where it lands from each bounce and at each wall hit it kicks up five puffs of the run-off's sand dust #d8c08a that spread and fade over 0.45 s, and at each wall hit eight flame-yellow sparks fly 1.5 m back over the track off the wall where the tyre strikes, fading over 0.3 s. The wall hits and the landings are read from the path the sim reports: a leg starting over, and half way through one.

Through the second the flag is up before a pass it waits, burning, on top of the wall it will roll in from, on the crossing's line, rocking 0.15 rad either way about its axle, and drops to the road as the pass starts. The sim reports the foot of that wall; the View finds the wall's top in the track's glTF. Up there no kart reaches it, and it hits nothing.

It is built in code like the items, not in the track's glTF, because it moves. The bounce is drawn only: the sim rolls it along the ground, so the reach it tests is the one the player sees where the wheel meets the road.

Hazard zone

assets/tracks/trackgen/layout.py hazard_zones, assets/tracks/build.py; apps/web/src/hazard-zone.ts
Size
Wall to wall, 18.4 m deep: 9.2 m either side of the crossing (WHEEL_LANDING_SPREAD_M plus WHEEL_RADIUS_M, the sidecar's hazard_half_depth_m)
Decided
the danger spec

Paint over the whole area the runaway wheel can reach: a 1 m band of chevrons across each end, 1 m stripes of gold #ffd23f and black #111111 leaning 0.6 m across the band, and a light yellow tint between, the gold at 14% over the asphalt (#807a70) and the sand (#ebc86b). Always there, so a racer learns the spot by the second lap; the flash says when.

Poster kart: Title runners and the podium

apps/web/src/poster.ts
Decided
ADR 0033, 0035, 0036, 0051, 0052

The kart side on, facing right, drawn from docs/kart-side.js: every face of kart.glb that shows from the kart's right, pitched down four degrees so each top face is a lit strip and the far wheels a sliver of tyre, shaded by its normal against one light in three tones of its material's colour, in painter order. Primary, secondary and accent as the View paints them, the carbon halo and pillar, the octagon wheels with their three spokes, the bare head in only the driver's parts. Nothing here is traced by hand: pnpm generate:poster rewrites it from the glb, and CI fails on a stale copy. The podium is the three cars you just raced, in their own palettes; the Title Scene's runners and the Room Scene's grid are the same drawing in the same livery, and a runner's wheels turn as it drives.

Don't draw a smooth wheel, a helmet or an accent hub on a poster, and don't paint a poster kart in its key colour alone; the poster car is the same car as the View's, in the same paint.
