Course Excitement Picks

What to add to Bell Deer, Goldstone and Monarch so each lap races and looks more like a Mario Kart course, without giving up the walled course (ADR 0022) or the F1 theme. Two analyses are merged here: mine and pi's (gpt-6-astra, read-only, same brief). Tick what you want built, add notes, then press Copy picks and paste them back to me.

The diagnosis

  • All three courses run on one recipe: 12 m asphalt, three rows of four item boxes, two boost pads, one runaway wheel crossing, ten coins on the centreline. What changes is the outline, not the racing.
  • The rewards sit on the default line. Centred pads and centreline coins mean there is rarely a choice between two lines, which is the core of Mario Kart course design.
  • Items arrive unevenly. Goldstone goes 937 m (71% of the lap) between its copse-exit row and its wellington row; its other two rows are only 106 m apart. An item rolls for 2.5 s, so a row just before a corner gives nothing for that corner. (Checked against the layouts and ITEM_ROLL_S.)
  • The scenery stands beside the road; the road never goes through it. Mario Kart laps move through contrasting spaces (terminal, apron, open sky; street, tunnel, harbour). Here the light, sound and enclosure stay the same all lap.
  • Goldstone's Becketts kerbs cancel the drift that should make the esses fun: four sausage kerbs in a row turn a rhythm section into an avoidance exercise.

How pi and I differ

We agree on the quick wins and on keeping the walls. Pi is more cautious: it would not add jumps to Goldstone or Monarch, new moving hazards, rain or wall riding until the cheap changes have been played. I think one shared, deterministic set piece per course is what makes a Mario Kart course memorable. Those items are marked contested with pi's objection under each one, and they sit in their own tier so you can decide one by one.

Things not to do

  1. No shortcuts through gaps in the walls. ADR 0022 settled it after play; every line choice here stays inside the walls and the checkpoint order. (Both)
  2. No second runaway wheel on a course. More crossings make queues, not excitement; the bot sweeps already showed bunching. (Both)
  3. No tyre walls back inside the Grand Hotel hairpin. ADR 0064 records the lost laps. (pi)
  4. No anti-gravity or gliding. Camera, physics, checkpoint and reconciliation work for something that fights the F1 theme. (Both)
  5. No density for its own sake. More rows of the same hoardings and trees hide the landmarks; strong silhouettes and gaps do more. (Both)
  6. No spectacle at a pit fork or merge. No boost, launch or moving object where a kart chooses the pit lane. (pi)
  7. No optional line that is always faster. Then it is just the new default line. (Both)
  8. Do not treat scenery as cover from items. The homing projectile follows spline progress; a tunnel wall does not stop it. (pi)
