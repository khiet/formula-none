Runaway wheel danger spec

The runaway wheel and its marshal post read as scenery: a grey wheel on grey asphalt (1.2:1) and a yellow flag over a yellow run-off (1.1:1). This spec records the redesign chosen to make them read as danger: a burning wheel almost three times the size, Oyaji as the marshal, and four tells that show the pass coming. The wheel no longer vanishes into the far wall: it bounces off the walls twice, landing somewhere new each time, so the approach is dangerous for the whole pass, and the cycle stretches to 10 s to leave a way through.

Behaviour

  • Rolls in, bounces twice off the walls and leaves (Bounce), turning by the ground it covers and hopping 0.5 m twice each crossing, the hop starting over at each wall hit. The hop is drawn only; the sim rolls it on the ground.
  • The flames always point away from the way it rolls, so they flip at each wall hit.
  • A kart whose box comes within 1.2 m of its centre spins out, by no one, as today. The bot's avoidance box (bot.rs, reach plus half the kart's length and width) grows with it.

Out of scope

  • Sound: the obstacles stay silent, as ADR 0041 left them.
  • The wheel on the course map.
  • More wheels or marshals on other corners, and the oval.
  • Oyaji anywhere but the marshal post.
  • Karts knocking the wheel off its path, and a wheel that slows or lies on the road.
